## The ruling

**A short VN scene on day 1 of a new game**, before the first calendar pick.
Existing VN dialogue layout, no new tech. A few beats, and it establishes
exactly three things:

1. **Why the mage came** — the premise hook: the reason this village, now.
2. **The festival stakes** — festival night named as the week's horizon.
3. **Player name entry** — the T13 name prompt lives inside the scene as a
   story beat, not a bare form
   ([2026-08-23-year-loop-saves-ruling.md](2026-08-23-year-loop-saves-ruling.md)).

**Deliberately absent: the year loop.** Roc left it out. The intro makes no
promise that years repeat — the player discovers the loop at the first rollover
screen. Do not seed it here.
