## Toby — the Giver

Decided primals: ***Needs-Me* driver** (Alive-high) + ***Interconnected*** secondary. Safe is **conditional** — safe only while useful. Enticing is **low for self**: a world of tasks, not wonder.

**a)** *The world is a place with things that need doing, and I am the pair of hands nearest to them.*

**b)** *The world holds together because someone keeps holding it. There has never been a moment it did not need me.*

**c)** *The world is safe while I am useful to it. Nothing is owed to me that I have not first paid for.*

**Notes.** (a) is closest to the *Needs-Me* driver in plain register and reads as reflex rather than complaint — it makes the conditional-Safe implicit instead of arguing it. (b) foregrounds *Interconnected* and sounds the most like belief rather than habit, but "there has never been a moment" edges toward self-pity, which his card avoids everywhere. (c) is the most explicit about conditional-Safe and pairs tightly with his conviction (*"He will not accept care he has not paid for"*) — but that tight pairing is also the risk: it restates the conviction rather than seeding it, and the schema wants the seed *upstream* of the essence, not a summary of it.

**If you want my read:** (a), with (c)'s second sentence appended if you want the conditional-Safe on the page. The schema allows two sentences.

## Ilsa — the Kinbound

Decided primals: **Safe bifurcated** — high within kin, low outside; ***Hierarchical*** as the organizing primal. Enticing **low outside family** — nothing beyond kin is worth-exploring. Alive **low** — relates by structure and duty, not intention.

**a)** *The world is divided into mine and everyone else's, and the line was drawn before I was born.*

**b)** *The world is safe inside the family and thin outside it. What is owed runs by blood, and it does not need saying.*

**c)** *The world is already arranged. Everyone has their place in it, and mine are the people I was given.*

**Notes.** (a) is the sharpest statement of bifurcated-Safe and gets the inherited quality — "before I was born" is the *given, not claimed* pole the theme sits against. (b) covers the most ground (bifurcated-Safe *and* Hierarchical *and* the unspoken quality her voice register depends on) but at two clauses it is doing the most work and reads slightly like analysis. (c) is the best fit for her **warmth invariant** — settled certainty rather than defensiveness — and matches her card's rule that any line reading as command or coldness is a defect. It is also the weakest on bifurcated-Safe.

**If you want my read:** (a) then (c) as the two-sentence form. (a) carries the boundary, (c) keeps the warmth. Alone, (a) risks reading colder than she is.

## Distinctness check

The schema requires each soul's seed to differ from every other soul's. Against the Keeper (soul A: ***not-Regenerative*** driver + ***Beautiful***, whose world does not give back and whose beauty lives in the past):

| Soul | The world is… | Axis it turns on |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Keeper** | not giving back; what was good is behind | **time** — loss is permanent |
| **Toby — Giver** | needing doing; safe while useful | **function** — worth is what you do |
| **Ilsa — Kinbound** | divided at birth; already arranged | **boundary** — who is inside the line |

Three different axes, no overlap. Whichever candidates you pick, that separation holds.
