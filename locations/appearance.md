# Location Appearance

Painted-backdrop looks and ready-to-paste prompts for the slice's interior locations. Added 2026-08-22 (Roc-approved). Built on the art-prompt preamble in [`../gdd/09-art-direction.md`](../gdd/09-art-direction.md); companion to the cast's [`../cast/appearance.md`](../cast/appearance.md). Location canon lives in [`../gdd/08-levels.md`](../gdd/08-levels.md).

## Two rules that shape these backdrops

- **Dressed, not empty.** These are authored places, full of character — the opposite of the home hub, which stays bare so the player can decorate it. The tavern and NPC homes are richly lived-in.
- **Character-free, UI-free.** Phaser draws dialogue and chrome on top, and NPC sprites composite in separately. So every backdrop is empty of people and carries no text or UI.

Camera is static and straight-on — the *Myst* living-diorama rule from `09`.

## What's canon

- **The Tavern** is an evening-only spot — "the Tavern corner opens in the evening" ([`08-levels.md`](../gdd/08-levels.md)). Warm lamplight, dusk windows.
- **NPC homes open at a bond level** — a reward for bonding, so they read as intimate and personal, not public.
- **A home's dressing is its owner's essence made visual.** Mara's "set for two" and Ilsa's extra places at the table come straight from the cast cards ([`../cast/`](../cast/)). That's what makes each read as *that soul's* home, not a generic interior. When a later life re-deals roles, the home re-dresses to whoever holds it.

## Style block

Prepend to every location prompt (the `09` world preamble, lively tone, 2026-08-22):

> Studio Ghibli style, hand-painted watercolor, warm and lively, gently saturated with rich natural color, cozy and whimsical, luminous soft light that fills the room. Warm woods, cream, honey and amber, antique gold, fresh sage and leaf green, soft sky tones. Glowing lamplight and daylight, touches of charming color — potted plants, flowers, warm textiles. Painterly Miyazaki warmth and wonder. No neon, no harsh photographic detail, no muddy darkness.

Every prompt ends: *viewed straight-on, static camera, full-frame background art, 16:9, no text, no UI, no characters.*
