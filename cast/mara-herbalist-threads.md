# Mara — Herbalist: thread registry (life one)

**Soul:** [`mara.md`](mara.md) — the card, which outlives this file.
**Role this life:** Herbalist. Role-goal: brew the festival tonic — the drink shared at the festival as a wish of good health for the year, from the season's last herbs gathered ahead of the frost. *(The frost stays her deadline; the tonic's meaning is health, not frost-warding — [`../gdd/00-world-bible.md`](../gdd/00-world-bible.md).)*
**Status: five ratified, one deferred, five rejected.** Authored 2026-08-09 as candidates for Roc's gate, after Toby's registry set the format ([`toby-baker-threads.md`](toby-baker-threads.md)). Roc's gate rulings of 2026-08-09 applied the same day: five candidates rejected (rows kept below as records), and the survivors plus two new rows ratified in the second pass, each row's **Reveals** column naming the distinct essence facet it opens — no two rows reveal the same thing, per the bar Toby's registry set. One thread has a downstream spec ([`../lantern-projects/v01/threads/mara-set-for-two.md`](../lantern-projects/v01/threads/mara-set-for-two.md)), authored before this registry existed; its row is marked and the ordering problem is flagged there rather than hidden.

**What a thread is here:** an open question this life keeps feeding. A caveat this soul forces, stated up front rather than discovered later: **Mara's role is plot-inert on purpose** (card `role_tag`, ratified 2026-08-07), so unlike Toby's feast, the Herbalist deal manufactures very little of her pressure. Her authored engine is **time itself** ([`../narrative-pipeline/mara-arc_draft.md`](../narrative-pipeline/mara-arc_draft.md)) — the festival week advancing, things fraying and going unclaimed — which is still per-life pressure (this week, this stall, this frost), just not role-made. Each row below says which engine feeds it. Only two are role-manufactured; if Roc reads "role-fed" strictly rather than "this-life-fed," the others are the rows to argue over.

**What a thread is, for the purposes of this list.** An open question the story keeps *feeding*, never one it resolves. It moves on new information. It is not the arc: the arc is what the threads add up to, and it resolves in the player's head rather than in a counter.

> **Ruling 2026-08-09 (Roc) — a role-goal is a situation, not a thread.** The feast, the centerpiece and the tonic are the **pressure** this life's deal manufactures; they belong in `delta_situation`, where they are uncapped and stateful, and they feed threads rather than occupying a row. The one licensed exception is a row that states the *tension between the want and the role-goal* rather than the goal itself — the arc doc's `toby-feast-short` is phrased exactly that way, "the Baker's feast can't be finished alone (the Giver's want × role-goal tension)". A row that merely restates the role-goal is rejected.
