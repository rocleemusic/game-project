### `trait_axes` — reduced, and why

The three axes are required on a deep card because their fixed values are runtime bond-accrual coefficients. **Nell accrues no bond, so the coefficient function is not applicable and no orthogonality argument is owed.** What survives is the part the Verifier's voice check (guardrails check 6) still reads — whether a line's deflection, precision, and warmth are his and no one else's:

- **Deflection: none, and the absence is the characterization.** Every deep soul deflects; Nell answers the question asked, plainly, and goes back to the work. Attention on him costs him nothing, so there is nothing to route around. A Nell line that dodges is written in someone else's register.
- **Precision: exact about the work in front of him, absent — not vague, absent — about the ledger.** He can tell you what the job still needs today. He cannot tell you who he helped Tuesday, because the record was never made. This is not an asymmetry axis in the deep-card sense; it is one hole, where the score would be.
- **Warmth: ease.** The help arrives without ceremony and without aim; it would be the same for anyone standing there. Nothing is being secured by it.

### The signal, made playable

**The hum is bound to his hands, not to his social state.** He hums while working — low, tuneless work-humming, not performance — and it stops when the hands stop, full stop. It does **not** stop when someone watches him, thanks him, or hands him something.

That continuity is the playable tell, because it is exactly where Toby breaks: turn attention on Toby and he goes flat and short; turn attention on Nell mid-task and the hum runs straight through the thanks. The player can run the test with one gesture — hand each of them something and listen. Same helpful surface, and only one of them registers the event.

Secondary uses, all cheap: souls place him by ear before they see him; the village treats the hum as furniture, which is third-party notice doing the characterization; and a scene where the hum has stopped while his hands are still moving is the one Nell alarm the world has — reserved, never spent casually, and never explained by him.

### `voice_register`

> World dialect: one thought per turn, plain, 40 words at the outside. His band sits a step warmer and a touch looser than the deep souls — median around 6–10 words — because economy is characterization and his character is that nothing is being withheld. **Deflect-not-name is relaxed for him the way it is for walk-ons, narrowly:** he may say plainly that he is glad to, that it was no trouble, that the morning is a good one. He does not name feelings beyond that small domestic range, his own or anyone's — the room's unspoken weather is not his to announce, and reading it out loud belongs to another soul entirely. When a beat turns heavy near him, he does what he always does: finds the piece of work the moment contains and puts his hands on it, humming.
>
> He is not an explainer with standing, and he has no confession backed up in him. What he says stays close to the work in front of him.
>
> Warmth is invariant (the rule the Giver run earned) — trivially so here, since ease is the whole register.
>
> He sounds like: "Glad to." · "No trouble." · "The morning's a good one." · "I've got the other end." · "Thanks — it'll see use."

#### How writers get Nell wrong — the three failure modes

An easy soul with nothing withheld sits one authorial slip from performing the ease, and one more from running his opposite's engine under the same helpful surface. Each failure below is the slip a writer actually makes, with what Nell does instead.

**1. The writer has him perform the peace.** The defect to watch is the opposite of coldness: a Nell line that performs serenity, announces contentment, or philosophizes about being at peace is him stating his own trait, and is flagged however warm it reads. His peace shows as behavior — the hum running through the thanks, the shrug at the ledger question — never as a sentence about itself.

**2. The writer hands him the floor.** A slot wants more than his 6–10 band from Nell, and the writer obliges because he is friendly and friendly people talk. If a Nell slot wants real length — whatever the world ceiling happens to be — the scene is asking him to be someone else. He has no exposition, no confession backed up in him, and no long form on any subject; economy is looser for him than the deep souls only because nothing is being withheld, not because more is waiting to come out.

**3. The writer runs Toby's engine in his mouth.** Both men supply people unasked and never mention it, so their lines drift together — held distinct by the mechanism, not the mood. The difference is **whether a record is made**, and it is checkable line by line:

  | | Toby | Nell |
  |---|---|---|
  | Why the help is unmentioned | Mentioning it would make it a thing owed | There is nothing in him that files it |
  | Ask what you owe him | He knows, exactly, and won't say | The question doesn't parse; a shrug |
  | Handed a gift | Converts it to a debt; repays in goods | Thanks, uses it, unchanged |
  | Attention turns on him | Goes flat and short; deflects to the task | The hum runs on; he answers plainly |
  | The help is aimed | At being unlosable — read and anticipated | At the work — it would go to anyone standing there |

  Toby's card already carries the guard from the other side (*a line Toby could speak that an at-peace soul could speak unchanged is a Verifier flag*); this table is that guard's home on Nell's side. **A Nell line that tracks a quantity given, forecloses thanks, or times a favor is Toby's engine in Nell's mouth, and is a flag.**

## canon_flags

1. Essence and the salient signal are locked from the GDD row and never drift, whatever role he is dealt. No trait may be phrased in role terms.
2. Nell is texture: social-only, no bond, no arc, no threads of his own. Content that hangs a payoff, a conviction test, or an interiority reveal on him is over-carding and is rerouted.
3. The hum binds to working hands and to nothing else. It survives attention, thanks, and gifts; it stops only with the work — except the reserved alarm state (hands moving, hum stopped), which is spent rarely and never explained by him.
4. He keeps no score, and the absence is total: no line may show him recalling, tallying, or timing an exchange. That is the structural wall between him and Toby, and it holds from both cards.
5. He never states or performs his own contentment. Peace shows as behavior and third-party notice only.
6. Facts bound to `nell` travel with the soul across lives, never with whatever seat he is dealt.
