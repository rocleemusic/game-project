### `voice_register`

> She speaks the world dialect: one thought per turn, plain, confirming facts and never feelings, with the world's 75-word dialogue ceiling (loosened 2026-08-23) as a cap rather than a target — her ordinary line sits in the world's 5–7 word band, and that placement does not move just because the ceiling did. What marks her is grammar. Her arrangements arrive already decided and are stated in the flat declarative present, with no proposal grammar and no question mark: she does not ask where you will sit, she tells you where you are sitting, and the telling is warm because the place was already yours. When a bond visibly fails to hold, she does not sharpen and does not speed up; she runs out of sentence. The declarative that has always carried her stops partway and nothing arrives to finish it, because her family never said an absence out loud and she has no sentence for one either.
>
> **She holds the floor only across long spans.** Given lineage or household history — whose table this was, which marriages built it, who sat where and for how many years — she can run long: that is exposition from the one person with standing to deliver it, and her long-span exactness made verbal. She has no long form anywhere near the absence. When the weight arrives she gives a fragment, then something her hands do — a reach that stops partway, a plate set and left — then a shorter fragment or nothing at all.
>
> She sounds like: "You'll sit here." · "That's yours." · "There's room by the door." · "You're on the bellows. The near end of the bench is yours." · "Put us down for it, then. That's hands enough, the two of us."

#### How writers get Ilsa wrong — the five failure modes

A soul whose voice is settled declaratives sits one authorial slip from sounding like a woman giving orders, and her wordless pole sits one slip from being explained into ordinary speech. Each failure below is the slip a writer actually makes, with what Ilsa does instead.

**1. The writer makes certainty a command.** The declarative present with no question mark reads, to a careless ear, like instruction — so the tempting line has her ordering people into chairs. **INVARIANT (stated with the spread, per the rule the Giver run earned):** certainty is never coldness and never instruction. Warmth is constant at every point on the spread; its channel is **inclusion** — she assumes you into the group, never welcomes you into it. At the settled-certainty pole she states arrangements as already true: declarative present, no proposal grammar, no question mark — "You'll sit here." "That's yours." — and the telling is warm because the place was already yours. A line where certainty reads bossy, brusque, clipped, or as issuing an order is a **defect**, however well it satisfies "settled."

**2. The writer fills the pause.** The sentence stops partway and the writer, unable to leave the gap, finishes it — an explanation, a trailing "…because Bram," a stage note about what she feels. The wordless-pause pole is not coldness, not curtness — *absence of available language*. She stops mid-arrangement because there is no settled sentence for a bond that didn't hold. The gap is the arc showing. She never fills it with an explanation, and neither may the writer.

**3. The writer gives the absence the long run.** Her 75-word licence looks like standing to finally say what the empty bench-end means. It is not: the run is available only for lineage or household history. Barred absolutely: any long run about the absence, about Bram, or anywhere near the wordless-pause end of her spread. A heavy beat near Ilsa is a fragment, then an action slot, then a shorter fragment or nothing at all — and what she puts in the action slot is always an observable act (a reach that stops partway, a plate set and left), never a narrated feeling. A grief beat written as a long line is a defect twice over: register and soul.

**4. The writer hands her another soul's pressure-tell or furniture.** Under weight, the easy moves to reach for are the ones the other deep souls own — held distinct by mechanism, not furnishing (added 2026-08-07, against the two in-band cards). Toby's instrument is tempo: he speeds up when attention points outward, goes flat and short when it turns back on him, and his sentences always finish. Mara's is tense: under weight her present-tense line goes thin while the detail relocates into the past tense, where she stays exact — pressure makes her *more* worded, elsewhere. Ilsa's is grammar: settled declaratives with the question mark missing, and under weight the sentence neither shortens nor relocates — it fails to finish, and nothing follows. So the three pressure-tells are a shorter sentence (Toby), a displaced sentence (Mara), and an incomplete one (Ilsa). A line whose pressure-tell either of the other two could carry unchanged is a Verifier flag, and the same test runs on furnishings: a shortfall converted to arithmetic is Toby's move, an object dated into the past is Mara's, and neither belongs in an Ilsa line.

**5. The writer writes a line Juno could speak.** Both women gather people, so a warm gathering line drifts between them unnoticed. Held distinct from Juno (Found-Family Keeper, also `older`, also gathers people): Juno *chose* everyone at her table and can say so; Ilsa was *born* to hers and has no reason to remark on belonging at all. Same warmth on the surface, opposite engine underneath. A line Ilsa could speak that Juno could speak unchanged is a Verifier flag.

## delta_rule and canon_flags

**`delta_rule`**

> *(Restated 2026-08-03 against the corrected count in [`guardrails.md`](../narrative-pipeline/guardrails.md) check 3. Her original wording already had the distinction right — public-versus-soul-bound — it just counted both sides.)*
>
> Every Ilsa scene declares one `delta_situation` and up to two `delta_cast` facts, with a floor of one new thing overall. A cast fact is a possession, a habit, an arrangement, or a line she does not cross; it is soul-bound and travels with Ilsa across lives regardless of the seat she is dealt. It is never a feeling, never an interpretation, and never a statement of her stance — "she values family" is bias-tier, not a fact. Situation is public and would be true whoever walked in, and it is **not counted or capped**: it is the cover the cast facts arrive under. **Two cast facts is a hard ceiling**, not a target, and not raisable by any agent downstream; if a scene's beat cannot fit inside two, that is a scene-spec defect to fix or surface, never a reason to add a third. Reference is free: the second apron, the empty bench-end and the centerpiece may be returned to in any later scene as often as the story needs, and none of that is a delta — it is how the kinbound-absence thread stays connected. What is flagged is a delta slot holding something already delivered and unchanged. Situation is stateful, so the centerpiece work advancing is a new `delta_situation` each time, not one restated. Whether a change is real or cosmetic is a human-gate call. Quiet beats carry no slots and are exempt from the floor, never from the ceiling.

**`canon_flags`**

1. **She is never converted.** The endpoint is *blood is tended*, never *blood is chosen*. Any brief resolving toward chosen-family collapses her into Juno and is rerouted.
2. **The conviction survives.** Her movement qualifies the belief; it does not flip it. "Family above all" is true of her at the end of the arc.
3. **The player is a witness.** No quest step, dialogue option, or scene outcome repairs the family or delivers the absent relative. Ease, never fix.
4. **Pressure is accumulated small absence.** No betrayal set-piece, no dramatic wound, no confrontation scene.
5. **Certainty is warm.** Any line where settled-certainty reads as command, coldness, or brusqueness is a defect regardless of how well it satisfies the spread. Warmth is invariant; channel is inclusion.
6. **The flat end is a pause, not a chill.** She loses the sentence; she does not harden.
7. **Nothing essence-side depends on `older` or on `blacksmith`.** She may be the postman at twenty next life and every essence field must still hold.
8. **She never states her own trait, and no World Truth is spoken in-scene.** Her blood-first stance is bias-tier; it shows as repeated behavior and third-party notice only.
9. **The bond score is host-side.** Never carded, never surfaced, never sub-scored.
10. **Juno separation is load-bearing.** Any interchangeable line between them is a Verifier flag. Same surface warmth, opposite engine.
11. **`arc_turn_bond_gate` is the only bond-gated echo.** Any second bond-gated payoff is drift.
12. **Name and gender do not re-deal.** Ilsa, female, every life.
13. **Bram is a Roc-authored fact (gate, 2026-07-25):** the family member who did not come to the arch raising is named Bram — name only; relation, reason, and backstory deliberately unstated.
14. **The set place is narrative, never mechanical (Roc, 2026-08-07).** A `gift` key item runs player → NPC only. Ilsa's warmth arriving as a plate already set produces no key item and writes no key-item record; any content that renders her inclusion as a mechanical gift is a defect.
