## H1 — NPC Roster

### Roster shape (locked)
- **~3 deep + 4–5 social-only** neighbors — a village of **~7–8**.
- **Deep** = bond-viable: full echo strands, ending-vignette variants, essence signature + all four card fields.
- **Social-only** = texture: cannot reach a deepest bond; one salient signal each.

### The foundation — the question the game makes the player feel (locked)
> **"What does *belonging* mean — and does connection outlive a lifetime?"**

Two linked axes: *what belonging is* (place / being-known / kin / chosen / pairing) and *whether a bond
persists past one life* (yes / no).

Design frame borrowed: **philosophy-as-foundation** ([`../knowledge-base/narrative/brennan-philosophy-as-foundation.md`](../knowledge-base/narrative/brennan-philosophy-as-foundation.md)) —
the cozy life-sim is the Trojan Horse; the belonging question is the payload; the player answers it
**through action** (where they spend their days = "what you spend time on is who you love"), never through a
lecture. Each deep NPC **embodies a distinct answer** to the question — a stance the player weighs, not a
trait-sheet.

Convergence that saves work: **an NPC's stance IS their uncrossable line** (`voice-style-guide` §5A.4).
Define the philosophy and the hardest card field is already written.

### Theme layer — the belonging research (folded in 2026-07-18)
Two KB artifacts now ground the theme (don't re-derive — read them):
[`../knowledge-base/synthesis/belonging-brief.md`](../knowledge-base/synthesis/belonging-brief.md) (western corpus)
· [`../knowledge-base/synthesis/belonging-across-lifetimes.md`](../knowledge-base/synthesis/belonging-across-lifetimes.md) (eastern / mythic).

**The answer to "does connection outlive a lifetime?" is deliberately layered, not resolved:**
- **Western** — a *fact re-found* (you forgot you belonged; the work is remembering).
- **Eastern / mythic** — a *bond continuously tended* (persists via karmic weight + active maintenance; the self is
  a stream, so it **re-forms, not reunites**).

The tension **is** the theme — **different NPCs embody different worldviews** (`belonging-brief` §4.10); our trio
does exactly that (worldview map below).

**Validation of what we already built:**
- The **repermutation mechanic literally is the Buddhist reading** — same souls, re-dealt bonds = the mental-stream
  *re-forming*, not a fixed soul reuniting.
- The **Keeper's two-state anchor is continuing-bonds psychology** (Klass 1996): the relationship doesn't end, it
  *changes* — present = tend the living bond · absent = tend the memory.

**Persistence system — met → claimed (LOCKED 2026-07-18).** The two imports are now locked as one system; they formalize
Sessions 7–9 (emergent bond · personality cards · superposition) under one vocabulary, and compose cleanly with the
parked letting-go ending (the wipe = releasing claimed bonds back to met / superposition).

**▸ Terminology (canonical, 2026-07-19):** the emergent quantity is **bond level** (per Soul). **"Partner" = the *deepest
bond*** — an emergent possibility, **never a prescribed romantic goal or a "winning" worldview** (pairing is one
belonging-stance among several). Legacy "emergent partner" / "solidification (of a bond)" phrasing throughout should read as
**bond level**. *(Note: "solidify [a name] later" is unrelated — that's name finalization, not the bond.)*
- **Claimed vs. met** = the cross-life **persistence rule** (`belonging-brief` §4.2). Acquaintances reset each life; a
  **claimed** bond carries a mark across lives — the concrete face of "connection outlives a lifetime," and the
  deepest bond is the ultimate claimed bond. **Claimed is *derived over time*, never automatic (Roc)** — no instant
  flip; a bond earns "claimed" gradually. Home: the personality cards / rumor graph.
- **Ritual as persistence** (`belonging-brief` §4.8) = the **mechanism** of that derivation. Repeated tending (the
  festival, gifts, a favorite meal, visiting a place) is what **accrues a bond met→claimed** — i.e. our locked "what you
  spend time on is who you love," now precisely grounded (time spent = ritual tending = gradual met→claimed promotion).

**Deferred — do NOT lock (Roc):** the **letting-go ending** (release the bond vs. clutch it; `belonging-brief` §4.9).

**Tone lexicon** (→ flag for a later `voice-style-guide` / `going-big` pass, not folded yet): **cynefin** (belonging
to a place you've never been — the theme in a word) · **natsukashii** (warm gratitude, *not* sad) · **mono no aware**
(transience-as-beauty) · **hiraeth / saudade** (absence-as-presence).

**Respect guardrail** (`belonging-across-lifetimes` §5): borrow the *shape* (karmic consequence, tended bonds),
invent our own mythology, credit the traditions; **don't frame more-lives as a reward** (our wipe = liberation,
aligns), and don't sell folk motifs (red thread, musubi) as scripture.

### ▸ Premise re-derivation — emergent bond, no prescribed partner (LOCKED 2026-07-20)
Retires the **"searching across lives for a lost partner / kept-promise true ending"** arc (the old §1/§6.1/§6.3 premise).
The game is **belonging-across-lives as an open theme**, not a prescribed search. Answers the 6-agent board's finding #1
(premise↔metaphysics contradiction) by retiring the *premise*, not the metaphysics. Refs: `heavens-vault-detective-story`,
`modular-characters-system-driven`.
- **Essence = FACT** (Obra-Dinn regime) — a Soul's signature is confirmable truth. **This is the retrospective-significance
  floor:** the plain detail detonates when you *deduce* the essence, so the engine fires on a fact, not a prescribed reunion.
  (Modular-characters makes each Soul a fixed essence-signature; Heaven's Vault gates its discovery.)
- **Deepest bond = EMERGENT SPECTRUM** — one *possible* ending among several (community · diffuse · solitary/release),
  never a target and never a threshold-you-win. "No prior fact before it collapses" is coherent now: bonds are genuinely
  constituted, and the floor lives on the essence side.
- **Theme (never surfaced):** what does belonging mean, and what is a bond, when lives are remembered across the cycle?
- **Recognition strengthened (Obra-Dinn deterrent, Roc 2026-07-20)** — 8 Souls is too small for a "large list" to deter
  brute force (board finding #2), so the gate stacks three: (a) **evidence-gate (Heaven's Vault)** — the assertion doesn't
  surface until the essence-evidence is held; (b) **compound assertion** — Soul × essence-facet × role-across-lives
  (Obra-Dinn distinct sub-picks), not a 1-of-8 pick; (c) **batch-lock, confirmation withheld** — recognition is the one
  carve-out from the informational-feedback law: no per-guess confirm/deny, you only learn on a locked batch.
- **Downstream:** retires kept-promise "true win" (H18), the prescribed-partner player-arc (H1 pairing), and premise
  §1/§6.1/§6.3 (Build GDD). The deepest-bond ending may still ship last for content reasons but never ranks above the others.

### ▸ Wow / lead — Disagreement B (RESOLVED 2026-07-20)
The board's open question — which single wow the slice leads with — is resolved: **the memory-cycle** (the world reincarnates
the same souls into new roles; the game remembers your lives and hands them back re-contextualized). It is the pitch wow
*and* the slice wow — **the same thing** — judged by the class definition (special/unique · gets someone excited · carried by
concept/feel/art, not system explanation). The board's feasibility worry (the cycle is the most expensive thing to
demonstrate in five weeks) is answered **not** by descoping the cycle to a scripted demo (the board's A/B fix) but by
**scoping the cycle SHORT** — a full life → rebirth → reshuffle lands inside the slice, for real. *(How short = a
prototype/§12 calibration; no number committed — Van Buren.)* Audio stays the strongest *supporting* USP (§8); recognition
is the interactive mechanic, not the lead. This also folds in Disagreement A / finding #3: the cycle is compressed, not cut.

### ▸ Primary audience & core loop — finding #5 (LOCKED 2026-07-20)
**Primary player = cozy-emotional.** The main thing people do is **collect and interact with NPCs, and see what happens as a
result of their decisions.** It rewards **explorers, collection-completionists, and people who love emotional stories.**
Deduction is **not** the draw — recognition/deduction *comes for free* as a byproduct of the metroidbrainia (using items and
spells to unlock areas). Recognition still delivers the memory-cycle wow, but it is tuned cozy, never a hardcore puzzle for
a deduction crowd. Resolves finding #5's "name the audience" half → unblocks the onboarding spec (written for *this* player,
inside the pull-not-push pillar) and aims the 3-page pitch.

**Onboarding (finding #5, other half) — RESOLVED 2026-07-20.** Onboarding just teaches how to play, and it already exists as
the level-layout **Opening** (variable entry + a distributed four-families tutorial cluster; each start-trio teaches the
whole grammar before its first gate, pull-not-push, via the `ignite` receiver-teach). The remember/loop reveal is
**discovered by playing, never taught in onboarding** — setting it up upfront would break retrospective significance.
This closes the board review: all four blocking findings, both disagreements, and every quick win are resolved.

### Worldview map — each deep NPC embodies a belonging theory (`belonging-brief` §4.10)
The trio isn't three flavors of grief; it's three **theories of belonging** the player weighs:
- **A · the Keeper → "belonging is *tended*"** (eastern / continuing-bonds) — kept only through ritual + care;
  present = tend the living bond, absent = tend the memory. The bond *re-forms*, never simply returns.
- **B · the Giver → embodies "belonging is *earned by being needed*"** (manufactured through usefulness — the
  *fitting-in* hustle, Brown §1.1). The ache underneath is to be *seen / claimed* apart from function (met-vs-claimed);
  the player's **"I see you"** is the corrective verb.
- **C · the Kinbound → embodies "belonging is *given* (blood, owed by birth)"** — fixed, to a fault. The world overturns
  the claim *around* C (blood re-deals; what persists is the tended, claimed bond — karma = care, not fate), **never
  inside C**: C's mind doesn't change, and the thread is *not the player's to resolve* (§5A.4).

The player's own arc **synthesizes** the corpus's reconciliation (`belonging-brief` §2): belong to yourself → be
seen by the right others → reciprocal claiming — constituting a claimed bond by tending (time spent) + being seen.

### NPC worldview lens — Primal World Beliefs (method, folded in 2026-07-18)
Source: [`../knowledge-base/narrative/primal-world-beliefs-npc-lens.md`](../knowledge-base/narrative/primal-world-beliefs-npc-lens.md) (Clifton 2019).
**An NPC's personality = the reasonable reaction to the world they *believe* they live in.** Profile each NPC on three
axes — **Safe · Enticing · Alive** (+ tertiary flavors) — and *derive* behavior, tone, and what they notice, instead of
stacking trait words. Concrete method for the A1 homogenization worry (distinctness = different *worldviews*, not
different voices) — and it **grounds the belonging worldviews we already set**: each stance is the prudent response to a
world-belief, which is also why each is (or isn't) shiftable.

**The three-axis profiles** (each secondary axis → the load-bearing tertiary that drives the character):

| Axis | **A · the Keeper** | **B · the Giver** | **C · the Kinbound** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Safe** *(vs. dangerous / unstable)* | **Low — *not-Regenerative*** (loss is permanent; the world doesn't give back — impermanence, *not* threat) | **Conditional / low baseline** — safe *only while useful* (+ *not-Cooperative, not-Abundant*) | **Bifurcated — high within kin, low outside**; **Hierarchical** is the organizing primal |
| **Enticing** *(vs. dull / barren)* | **Mid but time-displaced** — *Beautiful / Meaningful* live in the **past** | **Low for self** — a world of tasks, not wonder; attention all outward | **Low outside family** — nothing beyond kin is *Worth-Exploring* |
| **Alive** *(vs. mechanistic / indifferent)* | **Mid — localized *Intentional*** (the place "remembers") | **High — *Needs-Me*** ("I matter only because I'm needed") — the engine | **Low** — relates via *structure / duty*, not intention |

**Belief → behavior → shiftability:**
- **A** — *not-Regenerative* → **preserving is the only defense** → clings, keeps it as it was. **Shiftable:** repermutation disproves it (bonds re-form) → clutch→**transform**.
- **B** — *conditional-Safe + Needs-Me* → **manufacture indispensability** → over-gives, can't receive. **Shiftable:** being *claimed* unearned disproves conditional-Safe → B can receive.
- **C** — *blood-only-Safe + Hierarchical* → **family above all** → loyal to a fault. **Unshiftable:** blood-Safe is C's *only* ground, so it can't be safely questioned → *not the player's to resolve*.

**Attention differs for free** (Primals are Active filters): same scene — the Keeper notices what's *changed / lost*, the
Giver what *needs doing*, the Kinbound *who's owed what*. Free environmental-storytelling differentiation.

**Tertiary depth — driver + anchor (rule, agreed 2026-07-18).** Each tertiary gets a *job*, so it earns its place instead
of padding a spreadsheet. **Deep NPCs = 3 secondaries + 2 tertiaries; texture = 3 secondaries + 1** (their one salient
signal — no deep-profiling). Hard cap 2 (the note's flatten-warning).
- **① Driver** (already assigned) — the belief tied to arc / engagement: *shiftable* for A/B, *fixed* for C.
- **② Anchor** — one **stable** belief that never changes across lives; **uncorrelated** with the driver (§5A.2) and
  ideally a small *productive contradiction* that rounds the character. **The anchor doubles as an essence-signature** —
  a constant world-belief the player can recognize the soul by across role-shuffles. (For **C**, all tertiaries stay
  stable — no arc-hook.)

**Anchors (confirmed 2026-07-18):**
- **A · the Keeper → the world is *Beautiful*** *(Enticing)* — sees beauty, most in what's passing (= *mono no aware*):
  turns "griever" into "loves beauty, can't hold it." Recognition hook: *always finds the beauty in things.*
- **B · the Giver → the world is *Interconnected*** *(unaffiliated)* — everyone is woven together: the poignant
  contradiction is B *sees the whole web of connection yet won't be a receiver in it.* Recognition hook: *always the
  one who sees how people connect.*
- **C · the Kinbound → the world is *Pleasurable* (within kin)** *(Safe tertiary)* — genuine warmth / joy at the family
  table: humanizes the loyalist (immovable **and** the heart of the gathering). Recognition hook: *always gathers people
  to a table.*

**Roster-spread gap (for texture):** all three deep NPCs read **Enticing-low / Safe-compromised**, so cast some texture
neighbors **Enticing-high / Safe-high** (a wonder-seeker, a trusting optimist) for contrast and natural friction
(`primal-world-beliefs-npc-lens` [H1]).

**Watch-out (from the note):** the **Alive / "the world needs me"** primal is uniquely loaded here — our world literally
*is* authored and *is* the quest-giver, so a Needs-Me believer (esp. **B**) is *partly right* in-fiction. Mine for
uncanny effect; keep it inside the fiction, never a 4th-wall break (`dont-break-the-4th-wall`).

### Pairing / relationship — reserved for the player (locked; reframed 2026-07-20)
The **pairing** stance (belonging = one bond) is **one belonging-stance the player can reach emergently** — never a
prescribed goal and **not a "programmed true ending"** (the search-for-a-partner arc is retired). A deep NPC can't own it
without stealing the player's arc, so it stays **the player's** and is **mirrored in a texture neighbor** (the
soulmates-separated-by-timing couple, below).

### The mechanic — soul-cluster repermutation (show, don't tell) (locked)
Extends the locked **role-shuffle law** (`concept-dig-notes` S7: *roles fixed within a life; a new life
re-deals*) from **roles** to **relationships**: the same linked souls reincarnate **together**, but their
bonds **permute**.
- a **father & son** this life were **friends, not kin**, in another
- **soulmates** are **separately married** to other people this life — the same souls, missed by timing

The reincarnation link is **never told** — the player **notices** it: the same souls keep orbiting each
other in shifting configurations. Recognition deepens one level: *"same essence, new role"* → *"same
**bond-cluster**, new arrangement."* This is the diegetic proof that **connection outlives a lifetime** —
the answer to the foundational question, rendered as observation. It also powers **C**: repermutation is
exactly what refutes "blood is permanent" (this life's kin were strangers before) while vindicating the
chosen bond (the cluster persists regardless of role).

### Roster availability — the "past" slots (locked)
Each **lifetime**, fate deals **1–2 souls of the cluster as "past" — not available this life**: either **already
dead**, or **elsewhere / elsewhen** ("fate deemed them another place or another time"). The rest are present.
- **Every run carries a felt absence** — someone is always missing; loss is *structural*, not scripted. Which souls
  are absent is **generated** per lifetime.
- Feeds the **Keeper's two-state anchor** (present = shared connection · absent = reminder), the
  **retrospective-significance** engine (the absent *are* "the past"), and scope (a life instantiates ~6–7 of the ~7–8).
- Interplay with **repermutation**: present souls may be re-dealt into new relationships; absent souls are simply out this life.

### Deep card — A · roots / place  *(placeholder name: **the Keeper** — descriptive; solidify later)*

Roc's want (his words): A wants **a person *through* the place** — the tradition is the *anchor / representation*
of them (maybe where they met, or an iconic shared memory). **Recurring theme: A always loses that thing, so it
lives in the past tense** — the person or thing they love is already gone. **If the player fills the gap, it helps
A a lot.**

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Want** | To keep **the festival** — and the connection it anchors — from slipping into the past. Reads as *devotion* when their person is present this life, as *grief* when they're not (two states, below). |
| **Thematic contrast** | Belonging = roots / place — the *past-pole* of "does connection outlive a lifetime?" |
| **Uncrossable line** | The anchor stays exactly as it was — won't leave, won't let it be moved, updated, or painted over. Leaving = admitting the loss is final. |
| **Behavior cluster** *(proposed — repeated small acts, never stated: `voice-style-guide` §5, `realistic-growth`, `e04`)* | tends the anchor spot compulsively (re-sweeps what's already clean; keeps one thing set for someone who isn't coming) · speaks of the place in the **past tense** in the present ("This is where the lanterns *used* to hang") · redirects personal questions back to the place / its upkeep · keeps **one concrete possession** tied to the lost person-or-thing *(object varies with the generated loss)* |
| **Third-party seed** *(`e04` — someone notices, no one explains)* | "He keeps the corner set for two. Never asked why." |

**Variance dial** (keeps A distinct from B / C):
- **Deflection target** → *the place / its upkeep* (grief goes into maintaining the anchor)
- **Precision profile** → **exact about the past** (the date, the weather, where they sat), **vague about the present/future** (`voice-style-guide` §2.10 — precise about what's gone)
- **Warmth channel** → *holding space* — saving the seat, keeping the light on, making room
- **Talkativeness under weight** → terser, or drifts into upkeep-logistics to avoid the feeling

**Help-path — *absent* state (the "player fills the gap" hook — Roc):** the player can **reconstruct / return / witness** the lost
thing — find the object, learn the story, recreate the memory, or simply remember it *with* A — and it relieves A.
This makes A the game's **tutorial for witnessing-the-past as the love-language**: helping A rehearses the move the
player will later make for **a bond they come to claim**. Per the ending logic, the help given to A becomes an **echo** of
something the player once did for **that emergent deepest bond** (`voice-style-guide` §4; `concept-dig-notes` S7 ending beat).

**Two states — set by the availability roll (Roc — resolves #4):** the Keeper's linked person is dealt either
*present* or *past* this lifetime (see "Roster availability" above), and the festival anchor **flips meaning**:
- **Person present → the anchor is a *shared connection*.** Not grief — a living bond. The person may be
  **repermuted** into a new relationship (a neighbor now, not the spouse they were); the player's essence-recognition
  can help the Keeper *see* their person in the re-dealt stranger — the reunion beat, naturally motivated, best paid
  off at the reshuffle doorway.
- **Person past (absent) → the anchor is a *reminder*.** The behavior cluster above (past-tense, the kept seat)
  applies; the help-path is witnessing / memorial (below).

**Belonging-research refinements (2026-07-18):**
- **Worldview = "belonging is *tended*"** (continuing-bonds, Klass 1996). A personifies the **ritual-as-persistence**
  import: watching A tend the festival *is* the player learning the game's thesis — tending / time is what carries a
  bond across the reset. A is a **teacher of the mechanic**, not only a griever.
- **Enrich the behavior cluster** with grounded continuing-bonds forms (`belonging-across-lifetimes` §2): A **lives
  out the lost person's values** (keeps the festival the way *they* would have wanted), **talks to them** in passing,
  moves as if a **felt presence** is beside them.
- **Two-register tone palette** (maps to the two states): **absent** = *hiraeth / saudade* (the presence of absence);
  **present / healthy** = *natsukashii* (warm gratitude, **not** sad — "we were together on this"); wonder beats =
  *mono no aware* (the festival is lovelier *because* it passes). A is warm, not maudlin.
- **Shadow axis — clutch vs. transform** (Stroebe 2010: continuing bonds aren't universally adaptive). A's uncrossable
  line ("keep it exactly as it was") is the **clutching** pole; the theme's wisdom — the *melancholy of return*,
  connection is remade present-tense, not recovered by going back (`belonging-brief` §4.4) — is what A resists. The
  player's help can nudge A toward healthy **transformation**.
  - **Self-contained (re-examined 2026-07-18):** both poles live *inside* the tend-worldview, so A's within-life arc
    (help the Keeper move clutch→transform) is **authorable now** — no dependency on the parked letting-go ending.
  - **A is the letting-go ending's *counterweight*, not its dependent:** A embodies the *tended-bond* worldview
    (the bond transforms and persists — you don't let go), which is the pole that makes the player's eventual
    release-vs-hold meta-choice hard. A **feeds** the parked call; it works whichever way that call lands.
  - **Guardrail (protects the parked decision):** keep A's healthy pole = **transform** (the bond changes form and
    continues), **never *release*** — release stays the player's meta-choice, so A must not pre-empt or imply-lock
    the deferred letting-go ending.
- **Coherence:** A's linked person is a **claimed** bond (§4.2) — which is *why* it can re-form / return (present
  state); a merely-*met* bond would just reset.

**Retrospective-significance hook:** A's anchor object / kept-place is a **seed the player walks past early and
understands late** — the "where they met" plant pays off as a partner-echo (`voice-style-guide` §4: weight assigned

## H6 — Magic / Magic-Word System

### Locked going in (Session 6)
Memory-based magic: **components + the magic word = cast.** Spells are **knowledge** — unloseable, wipe-surviving,
carried in the player's head → **magic IS the metroidbrainia / knowledge-key system.** Components are foraged; spells are
learned through interaction *or discovered accidentally*; folk-spell collectibles; start persona = the mage ("collect
all magic"); persona gradients (mage: high mana, poor at art).

### The magic word — per-spell phrase (locked 2026-07-18)
Each spell is unlocked by **its own word or two, or a short phrase** — the phrase *is* the knowledge-key. Knowing the
phrase = having the spell; **collecting phrases = "collect all magic."** **Not** a combinatorial grammar (Roc rejected
the compositional-language option) — atomic per-spell phrases, though phrases may **share a root / rhyme** across related
spells as a soft family-resemblance (aids inference & discovery without becoming a grammar).

- **Casting = the phrase (knowledge) + components (materials).** Knowledge is wipe-surviving; components are foraged /
  consumable. The **receiver decides the outcome** (Session 8) — same phrase + components lands differently on a chicken,
  a door, a friend. **★ Physical outcomes only — LOCKED (Roc, 2026-07-19):** spells produce **physical** effects only and
  **never modify mood or behavior.** The receiver's *nature* decides the physical result — **including no effect** for an
  ill-fit target (ignite on a person = nothing). Any emotional/behavioral response is the receiver's *own* reaction to the
  physical effect (a cat purrs at a scritch), never the spell dictating a mood. *(Sharpens the H8 receiver-determined rule.)*
- **Naming style** (`voice-style-guide` §7): romantic-realistic, half-plain / half-evocative — *e.g. "still the water,"
  "wake the ember," "mend the seam."* Evocative **but inferable**: you can sometimes guess a spell's effect from its
  phrase, and confirming it is the discovery (proof-before-definition, §3).
- **Legibility scales with depth (locked 2026-07-18):** common / foraged folk spells are **transparent** (phrase ≈
  effect — cozy, low-friction; most magic is easy, protecting cozy-rhythm); rare / personal / family spells are
  **increasingly opaque** (a real inference, earned through close observation + relationship). This **welds legibility to
  belonging** — surface magic is legible to anyone, intimate magic (the Kinbound's family phrase, the Keeper's
  memory-phrase, the deepest bond's leitmotif-spell) is *earned through connection* (`voice-style-guide` §7 — legible
  either way; catching the etymology is a bonus, never a gate).

### Casting cost & limits (locked 2026-07-18)
Three gates + a quality dial — real friction, **no energy bar** (Session 5 preserved: mana is a *capacity*, not a
depleting pool).
1. **Knowledge** — you must know the phrase (the metroidbrainia gate).
2. **Components** — consumed on cast; forage to replenish (the consumable *cost*).
3. **Mana capacity** — every spell has a **mana requirement**; you can cast it only if your **capacity ≥ requirement**.
   Capacity is a static *tier*, not a draining bar. *(e.g. slice-bread = 1 mana → a 10-mana chef casts it freely;
   fireball = 100 mana → the chef **can't**, a 100-mana mage can.)* So **knowledge is necessary but not sufficient** —
   you can *know* a phrase you lack the capacity to cast.
4. **Quality** — mana headroom + persona gradient shape *how well* it casts: well within your tier = masterful; near your
   cap or off your persona's domain = **different-but-delightful, never failure** (the locked gradient rule).

**Falls out of this:**
- **NPCs cast different spell-tiers by capacity** → characterization (the chef's homey low-mana spells vs. the mage's big
  ones) and richer observe-and-learn (you watch different tiers of magic around the village).
- **The mage (slice persona, high mana)** can cast most of what they learn → fits "collect all magic."
- **Mana-capacity growth is a candidate progression axis** (grows as you awaken → bigger spells become castable) — hook
  only, not locked; ties to the awakening arc (Session 4).

### How you learn a phrase — never automatic (Roc, 2026-07-18)
**Core principle: magic is always *discovered / earned*, never handed over.** The world is full of people using magic;
you learn by paying attention. Four channels, none automatic:
1. **Observe & infer** — watch an NPC cast (their phrase + components + effect) and *piece the spell together yourself*.
   Watching gives clues, not the spell — you still confirm by trying it (discovery, not a handout).
2. **Contextual teaching** — an NPC may *offer* ("do you know…?") if they notice you **nearby** or the **dialogue is
   relevant** — emergent from proximity + relevance, never a quest-giver dispensing on demand.
3. **Found in the world** — inscribed, overheard, environmental.
4. **Accidental discovery** — try a heard phrase or a component combination and stumble onto a spell (trust-the-player).

**Double-duty (the elegant part):** each NPC uses magic consistent with their essence / role, so **watching someone cast
is also characterization** — you learn *a spell* and *read a person* in one act (Obra-Dinn-style observation welded to
the deduction/recognition core). What you notice depends on where you spend your day → magic collecting rides the
calendar loop for free.

### Knowledge-key gating (the metroidbrainia)
Knowing a phrase opens gates elsewhere — Myst-style, the key is *information*, not an item (Sessions 2/5). A phrase
learned in one context unlocks a puzzle / place in another; the spell list doubles as the progression graph.

### ★ The belonging weld (resolved 2026-07-18 — mixed, social lean)
**Magic is collectable solo, but people are its richest source.** The four channels deliver this: observe / find /
experiment carry the self-directed collector path (Sessions 6 & 8 preserved — no dialogue required), while **contextual
teaching** and **family spells** make relationship the deepest well. Collecting magic thus *pulls* toward connection
without ever gating on it — the same genre-alibi structure as the whole game (surface game complete solo; connection is
the reward). Per-NPC spell-gifts fall out: the **Giver** teaches by doing-for-you (love-language act), the **Kinbound**
guards a family phrase, the **Keeper**'s phrase is tied to the lost person. Folk spells passed down = "living out
another's values" / tended bonds (continuing-bonds); being taught = an act of **claiming / reciprocity** (§4.1).

### Spell list + components (Roc's content — in progress, 2026-07-19)
Physical-outcomes-only (rule above). Component taxonomy overlaps **H3** (foraged / consumable). **3 of the ~10 defined:**

| Phrase | Component(s) | Physical effect (receiver-determined) |
|---|---|---|
| **ignite** | **sticks** | catches **wood / fire** (lights a lantern, brazier, kindling) · **no effect** on a person or water. *(Starter spell — opening Use demo.)* |
| **scratch** | **wool** | on a **person** → removes an itch · on an **animal** (e.g. a cat) → gives them scritches (physical grooming; the purr is the cat's own reaction) |
| **breath** | **grass + dirt** | creates a **gust of wind**, blown at the target (scatters, cools, pushes light things) — physical only |

*Components confirmed so far: **sticks · wool · grass · dirt** → feed the **H3** forageable/component list + the layout's Collect/Make targets. Remaining ~7 spells = Roc's content.*

## H17 — Art Direction

### 2D vs 3D → 3D (locked 2026-07-18)
**Build in 3D.** Roc's rationale: **depth for free** (parallax without hand-painting it) + **reuse levels from different
angles** (one built environment yields many shots / scenes).

**Why this is a strong fit (not just a preference):**
- **Angle-reuse is a scope multiplier** — one 3D environment → many static-camera "scenes," which directly fights the
  content-budget problem (**H14**). Build once, shoot many.
- **The replayed festival week across years renders cheaply** — same 3D level, different angle / time-of-day / seasonal
  state = the "time moved / we returned" read for free (`voice-style-guide` §7A.4 — a signature landmark + a
  past-vs-present state shift, no intertitle). 3D reuse is exactly what this loop wants.
- **Fits the static-scene P&C "living diorama"** (Myst lineage — static cameras; Session 2) and the **Wwise**
  audio-first pipeline (spatial audio native in 3D).

**The risk + its mitigation (already in the KB):** 3D can read *sterile / un-Ghibli*. Hold the hand-painted warmth with
the `voice-style-guide` §7A lens — **desaturation discipline, painterly stylization, and cohesion-from-a-system** (No
Man's Sky model: hard-constrained palette bands + a locked silhouette vocabulary + one key-art board every generated
variant reads as a variant *of*). Cohesion comes from *rules + one review eye*, not per-asset hand-finishing (§7A.8).

### "Going big" — domain-mapped blend (locked 2026-07-18)
Not one global pole — each *domain* of a "big" moment gets the register that fits it (Roc):

| Domain | Pole(s) | What the "big" moment feels like |
|---|---|---|
| **Social / relationship** | **Frieren restraint** | payoffs land quiet — weight preloaded, a small word carries it, the gap holds the charge (never a swell) |
| **World** | **OW revelation + Ghibli awe** | the world opens up: an understanding recontextualizes (OW) *and* visual wonder / warmth (Ghibli) |
| **Magic** | **OW revelation + Ghibli awe** | discovery-aha (learning a knowledge-key) + the wonder of the cast / effect |
| **World philosophy** | **all three** | the deepest belonging / reincarnation revelations blend restraint · revelation · awe |

**Composes with the global rule** (`voice-style-guide` §4 / §7A): whatever the domain, **the words stay preloaded and
plain** — the swell is visual / scale / revelation, never a verbal one. The domain-map sets the *register* of the swell;
the layer-split (visual big, text flat) still governs the words.

**Sanity check against the cast:** the deep-NPC payoffs (Keeper reunion, Giver claimed) are *social* → Frieren-quiet;
learning a spell or reading the reincarnation truth via repermutation is *world / magic* → OW/Ghibli revelation-and-awe.
Coherent with everything built this session.

`interaction-beats = souls-present × years × days × beats/day` · `+ echo-strands × bond-deepening candidates`

### Day model + the scope lever (Roc, refined 2026-07-18)
- **A day = a budget of 3–5 screen-moves** (map switches) — the *attention economy* (Session 6: you can't be everywhere;

- **Each screen hosts X interactions** — solo (forage / collect / cast — **procedural, ~zero authoring**) and any **NPCs

- **NPC-availability is the narrative-scope cap.** Limiting *how many people are potentially available* (per screen /
  per run) is the knob that bounds authored content — the move-budget only **paces** how the player samples it; it
  doesn't add authoring. Composes with the availability layer's 1–2 "past."
- **Genre-alibi as a budget line:** the collector game is procedurally complete (solo = free); the authored story rides
  the social layer, and its size = **NPC-availability × interactions × years**.

### Worked first-pass (per canned path, real values)
- **Authored social interactions (upper bound):** ~6 available NPCs × ~4 interactions each / year × 3 years = **~72
  possible** — *the NPC-availability cap lowers this; the 3–5 daily move-budget just paces which the player samples.*
  Templated by essence → authored once per essence, not per permutation.
- **Major beats:** 3 festival nights + 1 ending vignette + reshuffle doorway = **~5**
- **Echo content:** ~1 candidate reaches a deepest bond per path → **1 echo-strand + ending-vignette**; the other 2 deep social-only
- **Descriptor-reaction templates:** ~6 present NPCs × ~5 reactions = **~30** *(templated by essence — authored once)*
- **≈ up to ~65 social beats + 1 echo-strand + ~30 reaction-templates per path.** × 2 canned paths ≈ **~130 social beats
  + 2 echo-strands** (heavy template / NPC overlap → net authoring well below 2×; solo days pull the real number down).
- **Systems:** **10 spells** · **~15 items** (3/category; gifts overlap) · **3 locations** in 3D, reused across years via
  angle / time-of-day / season states (H17 win: ~3 builds + state variants, *not* 9).

### Scope-reduction levers (what makes 6 weeks feasible)

## H15 — Audio-Tag Contract (audio-first USP) — spec-mode drafted 2026-07-19

*Sources: `gdd-structure-model` Build §8–9 · `dev-crew-architecture` §7 (Audio-Tag Agent) · `concept-dig-notes` S8 (audio-as-object) · S1 mirrored-tree.*

### The contract — department-agnostic GameplayTags + a tag→asset library (revised 2026-07-19, Roc)
**Supersedes the flat `<Entity>_<AnimVerb>_<State>` string + fixed enum + mirrored-directory auto-link.** The system is
**modular, data-driven, and department-agnostic** — one tag is a **game-wide gameplay-event key** that audio, text, and
art all resolve off.

- **Identity = a department-agnostic Unreal GameplayTag.** Each gameplay event is a **hierarchical, extensible GameplayTag
  with no department prefix** — `<Entity>.<Interaction>[.<Phase>]`, e.g. **`NPC.Chef.ShowAsk.React`** (*not* `Audio.NPC…`).
  One tag names one event, game-wide. The hierarchy grows by **adding tags as content grows** — no fixed enum, no schema
  change. *(This dissolves the earlier `<State>` enum question: a phase is just another optional tag segment.)*
- **A tag → asset library** (Unreal **DataAsset / DataTable**) resolves each tag **per department** — the *same* tag maps to
  a **Wwise event** (audio) · a **dialogue line/key** (text) · an **animation / VFX asset** (art) · etc. **One tag → N
  department resolutions**, so a single gameplay event drives sound + text + art together; **add a department = add a
  resolver column, not a new tag scheme.** Replaces path-mirroring; the library is the single source of truth (robust to
  asset moves/renames).
- **Direct event targeting.** Gameplay fires the event by **tag lookup**; each department reads its own resolution (audio
  posts its Wwise event) — **no Switch routing by default.**
- **Wwise Switches / States / RTPCs — reserved for genuine runtime variation only** ("if we get there"): e.g.
  **material-based footstep switching**, **change-over-time RTPCs** (intensity / proximity / time-of-day ramps). The
  exception, not the rule.

**Unchanged from before:**
- **Audio-as-object (S8):** sounds are **pack-free, free-traveling collectibles** — **show-able** (play a sound *to* an NPC
  = the leitmotif probe) · **gift-able** (a recorded melody = the declaration gift) · **spell-component candidate**.
  Deliberately recorded, never auto-saved; notebook text-writes stay separate.
- **Middleware = Wwise (locked 2026-07-19)** — Roc: more familiar + scripting; WAAPI is the natural home for library tooling/validation.
- **Ownership:** the tag **namespace is shared / game-wide**; the **Audio-Tag Agent** owns the **audio resolution** — it
  proposes compliant tags + maps/verifies each tag's **Wwise-event** entry (flags collisions + orphan/missing audio
  mappings). Generates **no audio**, assigns **no style/emotion**. Soft gate. *(Text/art resolutions are owned by their own
  department passes, keyed off the same tags.)*

## H16 — Notebook / Rumor-Graph — DATA MODEL (spec-mode drafted 2026-07-19)

**Roc's directive: the data structure matters more than the UI.** UI is **iterable** — inspiration: **Return of the Obra
Dinn** (assert-then-confirm deduction ledger) + **Outer Wilds** (auto-linking Ship-Log / Rumor-Mode node-graph with
"more-to-learn" markers). This spec fixes the **data**; presentation stays open. *(Sources: `concept-dig` S2 "log as central
artifact" · S8 personality cards · S7/S9 superposition · H1 persistence.)*

### Core entities (nodes)
- **Soul** — the persistent **essence-level identity** that survives every life / reshuffle. The **true primary key.** Carries:
  essence-signature (H1 anchor/world-belief), descriptors, leitmotif, awakening state, solidification score.
- **Role** — the **per-life** job/relationship a Soul is dealt (chef, blacksmith, brother). Bound to exactly one Soul per
  life; **re-dealt at life boundaries** (repermutation, H1). *Role is a costume; Soul is the person.*
- **Place** · **Item/Memento** (echo-carriers; pack-triaged) · **Sound** (free-traveling audio-object; carries the leitmotif)
  · **Moment/Event** (a witnessed beat — the log's atoms + echo seeds).
- **Descriptor** — an **essence-level** chip the player assigns to a Soul (gruff · always-finds-beauty · gathers-people).
  Revisable; wrong ones teach (informational-feedback law).

### Relationships (typed edges = the rumor graph)
- **Soul → Role** (this life) — the **recognition target** ("which re-dealt NPC is this Soul?").
- **Soul ↔ Soul** — bond-cluster links (father/son, soulmates) that **permute** across lives (H1).
- **Soul ↔ Place / Item / Sound / Moment** — association edges ("seen at the barn" · "keeps the ribbon" · "their leitmotif").
- **Descriptor → Soul** — essence tags (the card's contents). **Leitmotif(Sound) → Soul** — the audible essence-signature
  (deepest recognition clue).

### Personality card = a Soul-view projection (not a separate object)
One Soul's descriptors + essence-signature + recognition-hook (the constant H1 anchor) + filled/unfilled slots. Persists
across the role-shuffle — next life, a re-dealt NPC starts matching the card you filled for the "chef." **Card completeness =
the recognition progress.** *(Schema owned by the dev-crew Narrative Architect.)*

### Two data regimes (superposition rule, S7/S9 + H1 persistence)
- **Essence = FACT** (Obra-Dinn regime): a Soul's descriptors/signature are confirmable truth — assert → the game confirms at
  a match threshold. Wrong descriptors revisable, teach. **This regime is the retrospective-significance floor** — the plain
  detail detonates when you deduce the essence, so the engine fires on a *fact*.
- **Deepest bond = EMERGENT SPECTRUM** (constituted, not deduced): a per-Soul **bond score** accretes from time-spent /
  helps / festival-choices (met→claimed, H1). No prior fact before it collapses — **coherent now**, because the bond is
  **one possible ending among several** (community · diffuse · solitary/release), never a prescribed target and never a
  threshold-you-win. The floor lives on the essence side, above. Once claimed, fixed until save-wipe. *(Persistence engine,
  H10, owns the score + repermutation.)*
- **Awakening state** per Soul (locked / hinting / awakened) — symmetric amnesia.

### The Outer-Wilds layer (knowledge-as-progress)
The graph **is** the metroidbrainia progression graph (H6): edges appear as you **learn** them; each node can flag
**"more to learn here"** (un-surfaced connections) — the Rumor-Mode "unexplored" marker — without spoiling *what*. Knowledge
travels free (wipe-surviving); the graph is the visible face of the knowledge-key system.

### The auto-write log (S4 "writes itself")
Moments → log entries at a **significance tier**: quill-scratch (minor) · memory-motif (significant) · vignette (past-life
awakening). Plus a distinct **free-text notes** field (player's own words) that they can take notes in that will save with the notebook so they dont need to leave the game or have a piece of paper.

### Recognition — the core-skill loop, in data *(Obra-Dinn model, Roc 2026-07-19; deterrent strengthened 2026-07-20)*
1. Observe a re-dealt NPC (a Role) → assign Descriptors → build the Soul's card (essence evidence).
2. **Assert identity as a *compound* claim** — Soul × essence-facet × role-across-lives (Obra-Dinn's distinct sub-picks),
   never a bare 1-of-8 pick. **Anti-brute-force stacks three deterrents** (8 Souls is too small a space for a "large list"
   alone — the board's finding #2): (a) **evidence-gate (Heaven's Vault)** — the assertion doesn't surface until the
   prerequisite essence-evidence is held, so the menu can't be scanned; (b) the **compound space** — too many facet×role
   combinations to spam; (c) the assertion must be *earned*, never guessed.
3. **Confirmation is Obra-Dinn-style — feedback withheld:** correct assertions **lock in small batches** (no per-guess
   confirmation, so a lone guess can't be binary-searched or cycled) — best paid at the reshuffle doorway. **Recognition is
   the one carve-out from the informational-feedback law:** a wrong claim yields no confirm/deny, only the general
   **essence-vs-role** lesson (the costume red-herring, S7). A *performed* gate (design law), not a flag-checked pick.

### Open / next
- **Confirmation mechanic — RESOLVED (Roc, 2026-07-19; strengthened 2026-07-20):** Obra-Dinn **compound assertion** +
  Heaven's-Vault evidence-gate + batch-lock with confirmation withheld (above).
- **Detailed data schema (field types · cardinalities · keys) — deferred to build (Roc's call, 2026-07-19).** The typed-edge
  connections above are the **GDD-altitude contract**; the implementation schema is owned by the dev-crew Narrative

- **Descriptor↔layout** — which descriptors are *observable where* rides the **level-layout pass (game-34)**.
- **UI** — deliberately deferred; iterate later on the Obra-Dinn / Outer-Wilds inspirations. The data model above is the contract.

## H18 — Win / Lose + Format (spec-mode drafted 2026-07-19)

### No hard-lose (locked in concept)
"**You always leave with something**" (S3/S7) + the **informational-feedback law** (every action teaches) → **no fail state,
no game-over.** Soft-fail only: a wrong recognition or wrong gift is a gentle social beat that still yields info or a memory.
*The game cannot be lost, only lived — the theme, in the fail-model.*

### "Win" is layered along the nested clocks (S5/S7)
- **Run (festival-week):** no win/lose — the **festival outcome varies** by your lead-up choices + who you spend the night
  with; you always leave with the **ending vignette**.
- **Life (~3 festival-years):** reaching an **ending** resolves the life. Endings are **points on the belonging-spectrum**,
  none privileged — a **deepest-bond** ending, a **community** ending, a **diffuse** ending, a **solitary/release** ending —
  all honest. **Slice ships 1–2** — candidates: a warmer deepest-bond-enough variant + a quieter solitary/release variant.
- **The deepest-bond ending is one point on that spectrum, not a "true win"** (the old prescribed **kept-promise ending is
  retired**, 2026-07-20). It may still ship last for content reasons, but it never ranks above the others. The
  player-authored save-wipe (release → renewal) composes with the parked letting-go question (Build §12).

### The model = a spectrum of endings, not pass/fail
Success = **depth of connection reached** (which ending), never survival. Feeds the slice-contract "1–2 endings ship."

### Format = the Ending presentation format (Roc, 2026-07-19)
Endings are presented as a **short, audio-led cinematic vignette assembled from the run's data** — not a static end-screen.
Its parts (S7 ending-vignette · S3 15-sec memory-vignette = the fractal unit / polish target):
- **The festival-against-night tableau** — guaranteed; the vision varies by your choices.
- **The silhouette** — an empty figure that **fills in across plays** as essence-data accrues (face / mannerisms); the
  **job-outfit is a red herring** when data is thin (teaches essence-vs-role, costs nothing).
- **Echoes** — people you helped surface as echoes of things once done for the deepest bond; places / activities as shared moments.
- **The leitmotif** — the deepest bond's essence-signature **surfaces from the festival mix** as data accrues (audio-led).
- **Register** — composes with the going-big domain-map: social payoffs stay **Frieren-quiet**; the swell is visual / audio, the words stay plain.
- **Slice** — ships **1–2 vignette variants** (the "not this life" melancholy + a warmer deepest-bond-enough version).

**Win/lose + format locked** — no hard-lose · soft-fail teaches · endings-spectrum (slice ships 1–2) · **ending format = a
