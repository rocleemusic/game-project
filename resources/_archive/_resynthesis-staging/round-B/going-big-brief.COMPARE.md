# Going-Big Brief — Compare: Three Poles Side-by-Side, and Where Each Applies

**How to use this.** This is the merge pass for the Round-B going-big brief. It reads the
three divergent lenses — **L1 Outer Wilds REVELATION**, **L2 Ghibli AWE**, **L3 Frieren
RESTRAINT** — side by side, names where they agree, where they genuinely differ, and the
sharp trade-offs Roc should weigh. It does **not** pick a winning pole. Roc's D1b left the
pole **open to experiment** [`GATE-2-review.md` D1b; `RESYNTHESIS-PLAN.md` §2], so the
deliverable presents all three as live experiments and maps each to the places it fits.
The companion file `going-big-brief.md` (same folder) is the integrated brief the Phase-4
writer works from; this file is the reasoning behind it. **Nothing here is locked.** The
one editorial move made — a *layer-split* reconciliation of the "amplification destroys it"
tension — is stated openly in §4 and belongs to Roc to accept or remix at GATE 2.

---

## 1. The three poles in one line each

- **L1 — Outer Wilds REVELATION** [`going-big-brief.lens-1-ow-revelation.md`]: "big" is the
  cognitive detonation when a season of small, ordinary observations snaps into one vast
  understanding. It owns the **mechanism** — the knowledge-key metroidbrainia and
  retrospective significance already installed in the game
  [`curiosity-driven-exploration-outer-wilds`; `voice-style-guide` §4]. Its signature
  contribution is the **preload discipline**: a swell is authorized only when a named list
  of prior observations earned it.
- **L2 — Ghibli AWE** [`going-big-brief.lens-2-ghibli-awe.md`]: "big" is emotional
  amplitude in an ordinary frame — warmth, scale, a tableau that earns the word *sublime*,
  delivered through the **visual layer** [`ghibli-animation-secrets`;
  `secret-colors-of-studio-ghibli`]. Its signature contribution is the **four visual
  levers** (saturation-as-register, color-as-relationship, ambient-life, *ma*) and the
  keystone-image discipline [`essence-of-art-direction-mtg-ghibli`].
- **L3 — Frieren RESTRAINT** [`going-big-brief.lens-3-frieren-restraint.md`]: "big" is a
  vast scale made legible through stillness — the swell is earned by everything that did
  not swell before it [`frieren-hopeful-masterpiece`; `frieren-true-beauty-retrospective`].
  Its signature contribution is the **quiet-default register** and the honest naming of the
  "nothing happened" failure mode [`voice-style-guide` §10 Watch-out].

Each lens was assigned a different *layer* of the same moment — L1 the mechanism, L2 the
visual amplitude, L3 the register discipline — which is why the blend is largely additive,
not a tie-break. They are not three answers to one question; they are three answers to
three questions that a single going-big moment must satisfy at once.

## 2. Where the three poles agree (the load-bearing consensus)

These are unanimous across all three lenses and independently traceable to the locked
inputs. They carry the least risk and form the spine of the integrated brief.

1. **"Big" is not event size.** All three reject spectacle-for-its-own-sake. L1 makes
   bigness cognitive [`curiosity-driven-exploration-outer-wilds`]; L2 makes it emotional
   amplitude in an ordinary frame [`ghibli-animation-secrets`]; L3 makes it scale-through-
   stillness [`frieren-hopeful-masterpiece`]. This is exactly Roc's D1 read: keep the
   *function* of the tournament (contrast + an outlet to go big), drop the violence
   [`GATE-2-review.md` D1].

2. **Contrast is the mechanism, and rarity is the guard.** All three depend on a quiet
   default so a rare beat lands. L1: the detonation moments must be "rare and separated by
   long stretches of ordinary exploration." L2: "the swell is the rare mode that contrast
   makes possible." L3: "the quieter the default register, the more those motion beats
   land" [`frieren-hopeful-masterpiece`]. This is the same rule the voice guide already
   states as pacing-contrast: "a brief, decisive moment lands hard *because* it follows
   quiet" [`voice-style-guide` §6, `couple-seconds-to-end-a-fight`].

3. **The swell is spatial, not spoken.** All three deliver the payoff through the world,
   not through dialogue: a new space opening [`myst-techniques` 3.10], a final physical
   tableau [`myst-techniques` 2.9], a held background composition. None routes the swell
   through a narration box, an announced line, or a "this changes everything" beat. This is
   the shared reading of `voice-style-guide` §4's rule.

4. **Both D1a modes are real and different.** All three separate the **payoff swell** (rare,
   gated, tied to a narrative/reward moment) from the **sprinkled small wonder** (frequent,
   near-zero-cost, no payoff at the moment of encounter). The distinction is not decorative:
   the sprinkled wonder is the preload engine (L1), the ambient-awe proof that the world is
   real (L2), and the texture that keeps the player curious between payoffs (L3)
   [`GATE-2-review.md` D1a].

5. **Composition scale is a per-scene choice, not a hierarchy.** All three read D1a's "large
   scale OR intimate zoom" identically: the scale is the *frame the moment detonates inside*,
   not a measure of how big the moment is. A close detail can carry the same weight as a wide
   vista; Phase 3 chooses per scene [`GATE-2-review.md` D1a].

6. **Delta-storytelling is the cheapest going-big engine.** All three converge on the same
   near-free technique: the same landmark shown in a changed year-state — moss, weathering,
   a moved object — reads as elapsed time and scale at the cost of one background pass
   [`myst-techniques` 2.1; `pnc-grammar` §2.4 Ahnonay corollary; `frieren-backgrounds-analysis`].
   This is C3-legal — authored states, not a simulation [`RESYNTHESIS-PLAN.md` §2; `GATE-2-review.md` C3].

7. **The "amplification destroys it" rule is respected, not overridden.** None of the three
   lenses proposes ignoring `voice-style-guide` §4. Each reconciles with it differently
   (§4 below), but all three treat "weight is preloaded, not performed" as governing law for
   the text/voice layer [`voice-style-guide` §4].

## 4. The reconciliation — how a swell coexists with "amplification destroys it"

This is the sharp tension Roc named at GATE 2: the voice guide says *"weight is preloaded,
not performed — amplification at the payoff destroys it"* [`voice-style-guide` §4], and
D1a permits a swell. All three lenses resolve it, and they resolve it *compatibly*. The
brief adopts the union of their resolutions:

- **L1's resolution — timing/authorship.** The rule targets *announced* importance (the
  game deciding a beat should feel big). A sanctioned swell is not that; it is an
  *acknowledgment that the preload is complete* — the player's own understanding just
  changed, and the game confirms it minimally. The event is the player's felt convergence;
  the swell only marks it.

- **L2's resolution — the layer-split.** "Amplification through the voice layer destroys
  it. Amplification through the visual layer is the game." The voice rule governs dialogue,
  narration, text. The visual layer "carries what words are poor at"
  [`essence-of-art-direction-mtg-ghibli`] and can open into amplitude the voice cannot. The
  rule pair: *text stays understated through all payoffs; the visual layer earns one full
  breath at sanctioned payoffs; the two layers never amplify the same beat simultaneously.*

- **L3's resolution — swell = compositional opening, not scoring.** The permitted swell is
  "a held background composition that is slightly wider, slightly more open than anything
  the scene showed before" — the spatial reveal *is* the swell [`myst-techniques` 3.10;
  `pnc-grammar` §2.2]. No score hit, no NPC comment. *Ma* — the held beat — is the swell
  medium [`frieren-backgrounds-analysis`].

**These three are one resolution stated three ways.** The brief states it as a single rule:

> **A going-big swell is permitted when (1) a named preload earned it, (2) it lands in the
> visual/spatial layer, and (3) the text/voice layer stays silent or flat at that beat. A
> swell that adds a narration line, a "this matters" beat, or synchronized musical
> amplification on top of an already-amplified visual is the failure mode. Visual swell +
> understated text = the permitted mode.**

This is consistent with D1a ("permitted swell at narrative/reward payoffs") and with
`voice-style-guide` §4 read as a *text* rule rather than a total-register rule. It is Roc's
to accept or remix at GATE 2. **Flag:** this layer-split is the load-bearing editorial call
of the whole brief — if Roc reads §4 as a total-register prohibition, the "permitted swell"
in D1a collapses back to L3's understated-but-vast reading, and the brief's §4 rule reduces
to "compositional opening only, no visual amplitude." That fallback is fully specified in
the integrated brief and costs nothing to fall back to.

## 5. The sharpest trade-offs (what Roc should actually weigh)

These survive the blend — where the "right" call depends on Roc's judgment, not the sources.

1. **How much visual amplitude the cozy pillar can bear (L2 vs. L3).** L2 permits a
   saturation lift and a widened composition at the payoff; L3 warns that "still very good
   but competent detailed fantasy" produces a held moment that is pleasant but not
   revelatory [`frieren-backgrounds-analysis` Watch-out]. **Trade-off:** too much amplitude
   and the game stops being cozy-restrained; too little and the payoff reads as "nothing
   happened" (L3 Risk 3). This is a per-scene dial, not a global setting — the brief keeps
   it as a tunable, not a fixed value.

2. **Swell frequency vs. contrast erosion (all three, unresolved).** All three agree rarity
   is the guard but none fixes the budget. L2 offers an *untested* initial number — "one
   payoff swell per major gate, two or three sprinkled wonders per festival-week run"
   [`going-big-brief.lens-2-ghibli-awe.md` §6]. **Trade-off:** this is a Phase-3 art-direction
   call; the brief carries L2's number as a starting hypothesis, explicitly flagged untested.

3. **The static-scene timing problem (L2's flag).** A visual swell needs the player to be
   in the scene long enough to register it; a fast-moving player misses the held composition
   [`going-big-brief.lens-2-ghibli-awe.md` §6]. L3 reframes this as an *advantage* — in P&C
   the player self-creates the *ma* beat by pausing — *if* the background rewards the pause
   [`going-big-brief.lens-3-frieren-restraint.md` §3]. **Trade-off:** the design must make the
   payoff-swell room worth lingering in without pushing the player (the guidance-vs-confusion
   dial, `voice-style-guide` §6). Belongs in the pnc-grammar Phase-3 multi-goal-availability work.

4. **Preload verification depends on the scene map existing (L1's flag).** L1's preload
   discipline can't be verified until the full calendar + scene maps exist — the preload list
   for a Year-4 detonation depends on knowing what the player saw in Years 1–3
   [`going-big-brief.lens-1-ow-revelation.md` §6]. **Trade-off:** place the revelation-trigger
   markers early (cheap), fill the preload lists after the scene map is in place (Phase 3).
   The brief carries this as a sequencing instruction, not a blocker.

5. **Sprinkled wonder vs. sprinkled noise (L1 + L3).** Both warn that unresolved wonder
   seeds, if too many, read as confusion or train the player to stop looking
   [`curiosity-driven-exploration-outer-wilds` Watch-outs; `pnc-grammar` §3 diegetic-noise
   ratio]. **Trade-off:** the seed-to-detonation ratio is an explicit dial, not a maximum.
   Phase 3 tracks which seeds correspond to which eventual payoff.

## 6. Where each pole applies (the practical map)

This is the actionable output: for each D1a context, which pole's grammar leads and which
support. **This is not a pole lock** — it is a per-context assignment of which layer does
the most work at that beat. All three layers are present at every going-big moment; the
table names the *lead* [`GATE-2-review.md` D1b keeps the pole open].

| D1a context | Lead pole | Supporting poles | Composition (D1a) | Text/voice (D1a) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Knowledge-key payoff** — player connects a fragment across years (Teledahn cascade) | **L1** (the preload IS the moment) | L3 delivers it quietly; L2 supplies the palette lift | Large *or* intimate — per scene | Silent or one flat word [`voice-style-guide` §4] |
| **Retrospective-significance reveal** — an ordinary detail detonates | **L1 + L3** (mechanism + stillness) | L2 for the held frame's color | Usually intimate (the specific object) | No line; the player's pause is the *ma* beat |
| **Reincarnation echo lands** (partner motif) | **L1** (weight from another's attachment [`voice-style-guide` §4]) | L3 stillness; L2 tight composition | Intimate zoom | Silent; the echo is the delivery |
| **Year-cycle / meta-layer reward** | **L2** (the scheduled tableau swell) | L1 gates it (preload); L3 keeps text flat | Large-scale establishing, held | At most one plain NPC line |
| **New-room / reward-space reveal at a gate** | **L2 + L3** (composed swell, held) | L1 authorizes via the gate's knowledge-key | Large-scale (the space is the payoff) | No UI, no narration [`myst-techniques` 3.10] |
| **Sprinkled wonder — mid-scene beauty** | **L2** (ambient awe) *or* **L3** (object specificity) | L1 reframes it as a wonder-seed / preload | Both — intimate rewards *looking*, wide rewards *arriving* | Nothing; rewards the eye, not the ear |
| **Year-to-year environmental shift** | **L3** (delta as time-carrier) | L2 for palette-state continuity | Background state change, no new room | No intertitle [`myst-techniques` 2.1] |
| **Scene-entry establishing beat** | **L3** (linger before action) | L2 for the composition's amplitude | Wide "this is the world" shot | None; sets the register [`frieren-animation-breakdown`] |
| **Visible-but-inaccessible mystery** — off-frame implication | **L1** (produces the live question) | L3 ambient-life; L2 saturated lure-object | Either | None; the world is bigger than the frame [`myst-techniques` 2.6] |
| **Pack-triage / end-of-run** | **L3** (quiet, ambient, no ceremony) | L1 if the run earned a revelation | Background state of the square carries it | The "Thanks." template [`voice-style-guide` §4] |

**Reading the map:** L1 leads wherever the moment is *earned by accumulated knowledge*; L2
leads wherever the moment is a *scheduled, composed amplitude* (the rare tableau); L3 leads
wherever the moment is *ambient, entry-level, or a quiet payoff* — which is most scenes. The
default register across the whole game is L3; L1 supplies the mechanism that makes any
payoff mean something; L2 is the rare, budgeted breath. That distribution is the closest
thing to Roc's stated lean — "an Outer-Wilds mechanism rendered in a Ghibli register"
[`GATE-2-review.md` D1] — but the brief does **not** lock it, because D1b said experiment.

## 8. Thin spots / KB gaps (honest accounting for GATE 2)

- **The large-vs-intimate composition distinction rests on D1a directly, not on an
  independent KB technique.** All three lenses read it from Roc's decision
  [`GATE-2-review.md` D1a]; no KB note establishes it as a named craft move. Single-source
  by nature (it is a Roc-call). Phase 3 validates through scene drafts.
- **The swell-frequency budget is untested.** L2's "one swell per gate, 2–3 wonders per run"
  is a starting hypothesis with no source [`going-big-brief.lens-2-ghibli-awe.md` §6]. Named as
  an open dial, never a fixed number.
- **No sourced audio grammar for any pole.** L3 flags this directly: what ambient sound does
  at a *ma* beat, how a festival mix carries wonder vs. announces it, is not in these notes.
  H15 is ○○○ in the KB [`_index.md` §2 H15]. The sonic side of going-big is derived in Build
  §8/§7 from first principles or Roc's musical sense, not from the corpus.
- **"Held composition" is a film concept translated to static frames.** The Frieren visual
  reference is animated; for a truly static P&C the nearest equivalent is ambient animation
  (particles, light, environmental loop) + ambient audio. The method translates; the technique
  needs a translation step [`going-big-brief.lens-3-frieren-restraint.md` §8].
- **The layer-split reconciliation (§4) is the load-bearing editorial call.** It is
  well-supported (all three lenses gesture toward it) but it is an *interpretation* of
  `voice-style-guide` §4, not a quote from it. If Roc reads §4 as total-register, the brief
  falls back to L3's understated-but-vast reading — specified and free to adopt.
- **This compare does not resolve D1b.** Per the locked inputs, the pole stays open to
  experiment [`RESYNTHESIS-PLAN.md` §2; `GATE-2-review.md` D1b]. The where-each-applies map is
  a *recommended distribution*, not a lock. Roc chooses at GATE 2.
