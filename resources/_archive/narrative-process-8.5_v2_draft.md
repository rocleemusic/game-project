### 8.5 The Narrative Process

This section is the process for constructing the narrative pipeline: the documents the crew writes before anything generates, the order the agents run in, the guardrails that keep output coherent, the register every line obeys, and the loop Roc runs while building. Procedural technique comes first; cohesion and story serve it as guardrails. The constraint the whole process lives under is stated in full at 8.5.7.

#### 8.5.1 The Steering Layer (the arc doc)

One document per arc. An arc is a span of reshuffle-lives, a season. The arc doc is written before any scene generates, and it is what the orchestrator hands each worker as prepared context, the same job BioWare's half-hour theme-and-tent-pole one-pager did for a full team (storytelling-lessons-bioware.md). Its structure is ported from the campaign doc that ran this exact role at a live D&D table (D&D arc doc: CAMPAIGN_ARC.md). Five fields.

**World Truths.** Three to five hidden facts about the world the arc steers toward. Example shape for this game: "a bond re-forms across lives as a thing tended, not a memory stored." No scene states one. Every scene serves one. The player starts from zero and assembles each truth from the biased, partly contradictory accounts different souls give (npcs-with-agency-80-days.md). World Truths are not canon_flags: canon_flags enforce consistency, a new fact must not contradict a locked one; World Truths steer direction, they tell Intake which fact to plant next (D&D arc doc: World Truths). The game's philosophical layer lives inside this field, never spoken aloud, answered only by how the player spends their time (D&D arc doc: Philosophical Thread; brennan-philosophy-as-foundation.md).

**The Arc Question.** One per arc. Example: "across these three festival-years, does this soul become someone the player tends, or someone the player releases?" It gives every per-scene delta a direction. The prior-art evidence is blunt: the campaign arc that had a question felt like it was building, and the stretch without one read as "and then, and then" (D&D arc doc: NARRATIVE_NOTES.md). The question is answered by play. It is never resolved in dialogue and never tested for comprehension. One question only; a second question competing for the delta's direction is over-authoring, and the philosophical layer folds into the World Truths instead (D&D arc doc: Philosophical Thread).

**Soul Arc Spines.** One line per soul, from X to Y across the arc's reshuffle-lives: Mara, from keeper to giver (D&D arc doc: Arc Spines). The spine holds the one job no other field covers: direction of change for a single soul over time. Essence is fixed by design, the same want in every life; the Arc Question is one question for the whole arc; World Truths are facts about the world. None of those can order one soul's content across lives. The spine can: it biases which encounters and echo seeds exist for that soul early in the arc versus late, so Mara's life-one pool and her life-three pool are not interchangeable. It never gates content on reaching Y, and it never drives the bond score toward a destination: per section 8.3 the deepest bond stays emergent, one ending among several (community, diffuse, solitary or release), and a soul whose spine points at giving can still end at any of the three.

**Threads to Not Drop.** A staleness list. Each open thread carries a last-moved counter; any thread untouched for six scenes biases the next scene request toward moving it. Move means new information on the thread, not resolution (D&D arc doc: Threads to Not Drop). The cap counts scenes, tuned to player memory rather than simulated time (emergent-storytelling-the-sims.md), and it supplies the "not sent there lately" axis the errand generator reads (heavens-vault-detective-story.md). A thread shown at the edge of frame must stay interpretable and must eventually move; shown-then-abandoned is a defect.

**What This Arc Is NOT.** Explicit anti-goals, so an omission is a decision instead of a silent gap (D&D arc doc: What This Season Is NOT). The list includes omitted verbs, because the actions we leave out characterize this world as much as the ones we keep (storytelling-with-verbs.md). At Intake the list is a filter: a scene brief that drifts into an anti-goal is rerouted before it reaches prose.

The steering layer steers content, never a feeling. Every field is a world fact, a direction, or a counter. A World Truth phrased as a player outcome ("the player feels the bond persists") is rejected at the doc stage.

#### 8.5.2 The Generation Procedure

The pipeline runs in four phases and never mixes them: arc and beat map, then scene graph, then scaffold, then lines (efficiently-branching-narrative.md). The Narrative Architect (Agent 1) owns the first two phases; the Content Agent (Agent 2) owns only the last. The steps are:

**1. Steering.** Write or refresh the arc doc (8.5.1). Nothing generates without it.

**2. Intake.** Four inputs: the arc doc; Roc's roster of NPC seeds (the Architect cannot invent a soul); a backstory guideline per soul (D&D arc doc: NPC postures); and the player-chosen background. The background seeds the player's own backstory and a proposed player arc, the way a D&D player hands the DM a backstory (D&D arc doc: player spines). The pipeline steers that arc with the softest mediation only: surface a clue, shift a neighbor, offer a lead. It never fails a player action to herd them back onto the proposed arc, and it never buries the arc so it cannot surface; both stronger mediations are push and are barred by name (procedural-narrative-generation.md). Intake resolves the current state into a bag of plain facts and structural scores: confirmed essence descriptors, knowledge held, world state, festival year, the reshuffle role map, the hidden bond score. Nothing felt ever enters the bag (Fallen London / StoryNexus). Every scene request leaves Intake tagged with the one World Truth it serves, the Arc Question, any spine in play, and any stale thread it should move. A request with no steering tag bounces back to Intake; no scene generates without reference to the arc layer.

**3. Cards.** The Architect fills each seed and its backstory guideline into a persona_card. The essence_descriptor is one want, one behavior cluster, one thematic contrast to the player's arc, stated as want and repeated action, never as a job (creating-strong-characters.md; modular-characters-system-driven.md). Trait axes are orthogonal by rule: deflection target, precision profile, and warmth channel are independent, and a value on one never predicts a value on another, because correlated traits are what make a generated cast read as one archetype in different hats (voice-style-guide.md; modular-characters-system-driven.md). Each card tags to one suit from a small hand-set so thematic links across souls are placed on purpose. Each card also carries a voice_register, stable across reshuffles; one conviction no bond state can buy out (npcs-with-agency-80-days.md); a note on what this soul notices and wants, written before any line exists (romantic-narrative-design-kindness-coins.md); and its Soul Arc Spine. Five to ten percent of the roster is authored_exceptions: handwritten souls that break these rules, marked on the schema so the Verifier reads them as sanctioned (modular-characters-system-driven.md). Scope stays honest: a few deep souls carry full state, the rest run lighter (narrative-lego-ken-levine.md).

**4. Echoes.** The Architect writes echo_templates: seed_scene, seed_event under 25 words, payoff_scene, payoff_condition. The seed sits beside plot-inert material, a chore, a price, a pest, so it cannot be told from noise when planted (when-great-foreshadowing-goes-unnoticed.md; e02.md). It names a thing the player can picture, a worn path, a drafty seat, because a vague seed cannot be recognized later (environment-as-visual-storytelling.md). The payoff_condition is a named deduction the player must already hold: a recognition condition, never a promise about feeling. Turn shapes come from the Frieren templates: the cut-off with a deferred gap, the logistics-first reveal, the motif that recurs near-verbatim in a heavier register (frieren-dialogue-jp.md). Echoes tier by inference load, surface, mid, hidden, with placement carrying the tier and no difficulty label (curiosity-driven-exploration-outer-wilds.md). Each template is conditioned on history, so the same template pays off differently depending on the soul's card, the accrued bond, and the role they held last life (Wildermyth). Two bounds hold: the seed-to-payoff ratio is tracked at arc level, because seeds that never land train the player to stop looking (when-great-foreshadowing-goes-unnoticed.md), and every seed scene must work on its own for a player who never returns (when-a-scene-is-more-tragic-than-you-realized.md). A payoff that reaches into a soul's past must recontextualize something present, or it is a dump and fails its worth check (the-problem-with-backstory.md).

**5. Delta and canon.** The Architect sets the delta_rule: every scene adds one new fact, and a scene that restates a known fact is rejected. The richer form, ported from the table: a scene may carry one new WORLD fact (a World Truth getting more visible) and one new PERSONAL fact (a soul fact getting more specific: a possession, a habit, a line they will not cross), in the same scene when possible (D&D arc doc: NARRATIVE_NOTES.md). Both are facts revealed. "The player feels closer" is not a fact and never fills the slot. The facts arrive under cover: a scene's surface job is a favor, a joke, a small conflict, and a scene whose only job is exposition is the failure mode (in-praise-of-layered-exposition.md). When a past choice resurfaces after the year skip, its consequence shows as a callback line and a changed piece of world, never a signposted right answer (quest-design-lessons-witcher-cyberpunk.md). A quiet beat with no new fact is a valid, scheduled beat type, not a gap to fill; the breathing room is what later recognition needs (in-pursuit-of-a-perfect-montage.md). canon_flags lock what must not drift, including world facts bound to a soul's ID, so an established fact travels with the soul into their next role and never passes to whoever now holds the old seat (side-characters-to-worldbuild.md).

**6. Graph before prose.** The scene graph goes up greybox first: one named node per scene beat, placeholder lines carrying full branch logic, no finished prose (narrative-games-yarn-spinner.md). Content the player has seen gates later content automatically, so every prior beat is a future condition without a hand-kept flag table (narrative-games-yarn-spinner.md). Scenes assemble from small preconditioned pieces rather than fixed trees, each piece gated on world state and knowledge state, each safe to enter in any order with a zero-information fallback, so significance survives being found first, last, or alone (Fallen London / StoryNexus; heavens-vault-detective-story.md). Encounters, not quests: errands generate from world state by triangulating three inputs, what fits this place, what the arc doc needs surfaced next, and what the player has not been offered lately, and the output is always two or three live leads, never one marker (heavens-vault-detective-story.md). A pacing layer above the pool biases which pieces surface, and only biases; it never forces a beat the player did not walk toward (RimWorld). The errand vocabulary stays small and hand-curated, or triangulation produces junk (heavens-vault-detective-story.md).

**7. Recognition gates.** Every essence-side gate, the deduction the player can win, is specced in five fields: Problem, Circumstance, Clues, Solution, and The Idea, one plain sentence of intent that survives the trip from Architect to Content Agent to Verifier to Roc (myst-techniques.md). The proof is diegetic: an action only someone who understood would choose, never a button that says "I figured it out" (narrative-deduction-mechanics.md). The gate is built as an argument assembled piece by piece, so wrong guesses walk back and teach, and "inconclusive, come back when you know more" is a legitimate outcome, not a failure (narrative-deduction-mechanics.md). Gate and verbs are co-designed: one of the four verb families, Collect, Make, Use, Converse (Show and Ask are tag-layer names under Use and Converse), must be able to carry the proof, or the gate collapses into a menu. The families are this document's own verb grammar, defined in sections 5 and 6.1; the co-design rule comes from storytelling-with-verbs.md. The verb set locks after the first festival year; stakes escalate across the three years, the verbs do not, and the run's retrospective resolves through the deduction the player already practiced (mechanics-elevate-narrative-oxenfree.md).

**8. Lines, one slot per call.** The Content Agent receives one prepared slot: persona_card, echo_template, scene_context, an assigned tone from the fixed enum (quiet, wistful, matter_of_fact, warm, distant), voice_register, and max_words. It fills a chosen turn scaffold rather than free-writing from a blank page (frieren-dialogue-jp.md), substituting card values into the scaffold's named slots so the suit and essence signature run through every line the soul speaks (procedural-history-caves-of-qud.md). Each output line carries a speaker_intent note: what the line means from the speaker's side, care routed through maintenance, grief surfacing as a price list (dialogue-of-hades.md). The agent makes no structural decision, assigns no tone of its own, and invents no soul, trait, or fact not on the card. Its context is rich and its output is compressed: the card carries far more than any line states, and the line spends the fewest exact words (how-to-write-absurdly-well-tchaikovsky.md). Against drift over a long batch, the orchestrator re-pins a compressed card summary and the voice_register block into every call, and re-summarizes accumulated scene state every ten slots.

**9. Cross-run state.** Each soul has a canon store that persists across runs and reshuffles: essence_descriptor, signature, locked facts. The dead sister stays dead in every life. A soul is never regenerated fresh per run; a reshuffle deals a new role and a subset of the soul's authored facets from its pool, essence fixed (narrative-lego-ken-levine.md). Confirmed essence descriptors live where they belong, in the player's head and a diegetic almanac the player fills, re-asserted against the same soul across lives as the player's own act (visual-novel-design-virtues-last-reward.md). The per-soul bond score accretes silently, host-side, from time spent, help given, festival choices. It has no UI. No hearts, no meter, no "bond deepened" event, no quest-log line naming a want. It surfaces only as behavior: a changed line, a door left open, a soul that could not hold back. The threshold beat is the soul's own act, never a payout for niceness (romantic-narrative-design-kindness-coins.md). Wants read as behavior and dialogue; the motive underneath stays open, a surface the player projects onto, while the feedback to any specific player action stays concrete (emergent-storytelling-the-sims.md). At the retrospective the accreted score selects among the several endings; it is never printed, and it never fires one mandated scene (narrative-games-yarn-spinner.md).

**10. Verify.** The Consistency Verifier (Agent 3) reads each batch against the invariant set in 8.5.3 and against every soul's canon store. This includes the coherence gate the replacement grammar needs: a grammar-filled line tends to explain itself after the fact, so session self-consistency is checked, not assumed (procedural-history-caves-of-qud.md). The Verifier flags only. It never rewrites (dev-crew-architecture.md).

**11. Purge the tells.** Before the human gate, a mechanical editing pass runs on every batch: em-dashes out, banned words scanned, summary openers cut, passive voice flagged, and a specificity check, every clause names a thing the player can picture or the clause goes (environment-as-visual-storytelling.md). It flags like the Verifier; it does not rewrite.

**12. QA.** QA (Agent 5) runs reachability, soft-lock, and dead-end checks over the scene graph, plus spot checks that a wrong action teaches (non-linear-narratives-horizon.md). Flags only.

**13. Escalate, then gate.** A flag on prose returns to the Content Agent for one revision inside the same canon. A flag naming a structural cause, a bad echo_template, an essence contradiction, routes through the orchestrator up to the Architect as a new prepared input; workers never call each other. Anything still flagged after one revision rides to the gate with its flag attached. Roc approves every line at the output. The gate does not move mid-chain.

**A worked pass: one soul, two lives.**

Card, abridged. Mara. Want: keep things from being lost. Behavior cluster: mends small broken things unasked; keeps a drawer of objects nobody claimed. Contrast: the player passes through lives, Mara holds on. Axes: deflects to logistics; exact about objects and prices, vague about people; warmth shows as effort. Suit: keeping. voice_register: short clauses, price-talk, never names a feeling. Conviction: the child's whistle in the drawer is not for sale. Spine: from keeper to giver. Locked canon: her sister died the winter before the player first arrived. For contrast, a second card: Odo. Want: to be witnessed. Behavior cluster: retells the same three stories, one detail changed each year; deflects to jokes; warmth shows as accommodation. The two are not swappable by changing a noun.

Life one, Mara is the blacksmith. Scene N delta: WORLD, the festival lanterns are folded from letters the dead never received; PERSONAL, Mara keeps the drawer. Her line, matter_of_fact: "Nails are three a copper. Leave the gate hinge, I did it this morning." The seed_event, seventeen words, planted beside a price: "Mara oils the school gate hinge before opening her stall, charges nobody, and does not mention it." speaker_intent: care routed through maintenance. Scene N+1 delta: WORLD, the well rope has frayed another strand since last year (D&D arc doc: NARRATIVE_NOTES.md, show the change); PERSONAL, one drawer object is a child's whistle she will not sell. Both facts are new; neither restates.

Life two, after the reshuffle, Mara is the postman. The canon store holds: the drawer travels with her, the sister is still dead, the prices are still exact. Her late line, same register, same clause economy: "Letters run late festival week. Leave the gate, I did it this morning." The near-verbatim recurrence is the motif shape (frieren-dialogue-jp.md). The echo pays off once the payoff_condition is met: the player has confirmed her essence in a prior life and has read the seed. The payoff line belongs to a third party, the schoolteacher, flat: "That gate has never squeaked. Nobody ever knew who." Weight is assigned from someone else's attachment, at minimum length (e01.md; e14.md). No narration adds anything. If the moment earns a swell, the camera holds on the gate and the sound bed lifts; the text stays silent (voice-style-guide.md). A life-two line that gives Mara a living sister, or a vague price, is a Verifier flag against her store.

#### 8.5.3 Cohesion Guardrails

The Verifier checks a finite, locked list. The list exists before the first card is written, because "consistent" has no definition until it does (dev-crew-architecture.md). The Verifier is structural only: it checks facts, boundaries, and grammar, and it never asks whether a line feels right, because judging resonance is measuring it. Authored exceptions are marked on the card, so a sanctioned rule-break is never a flag.

1. **Essence vs role.** No trait attaches to the job. "Gruff blacksmith" is a defect; the blacksmith may be the postman next life, and the essence must read true either way (creating-strong-characters.md). The check is mechanical: an essence_descriptor, card note, or line that phrases a trait in role terms is flagged. Whether the reshuffle lands well is the human gate's call (8.5.6); the Verifier checks the wording only. This is the highest-stakes check in the pipeline: if a role ever stands in for an essence, the winnable deduction quietly breaks.

2. **Superposition.** The essence side is fact: assertable, confirmable at a threshold, revisable. The bond side is emergent: hidden, accreted, one of several endings. Neither feeds the other. Bond never gates an essence fact; a confirmed essence never adds bond. Section 8.3 sets this; the check enforces it.

3. **Delta.** Each scene delivers its declared fact or facts. A scene that paraphrases a prior fact is flagged. A personal fact is a fact: a possession, a habit, a line not crossed. A personal slot phrased as a player feeling is flagged (D&D arc doc: NARRATIVE_NOTES.md).

4. **Knowledge travels.** A fact learned in one scene or one life persists correctly into the next. Soul-bound facts move with the soul, never with the vacated seat (side-characters-to-worldbuild.md).

5. **Feedback vs motive.** The feedback to a player action is specific and teaching; a wrong deduction attempt still yields something usable (narrative-deduction-mechanics.md). The soul's motive stays open. Spelling out the inner life is a flag; vague feedback is also a flag (emergent-storytelling-the-sims.md).

6. **Voice register.** Two separate line items, never collapsed: does the line obey the shared world dialect, short, deflecting, wrong-register; and do its specific deflection, precision, and warmth match this soul's card and no other's (voice-style-guide.md). One collapsed check is how a homogenized cast slips through.

7. **Fact tier vs bias tier.** Locked canon and confirmed essence are fact-tier. A soul's stance on a World Truth is bias-tier: partial, slanted, allowed to contradict another soul's. A line that promotes a bias-tier stance to stated fact, or states a World Truth outright, is flagged; each batch must serve its assigned truth without saying it (npcs-with-agency-80-days.md; D&D arc doc: World Truths).

**The speaker_intent guard.** speaker_intent describes the speaker only: what the line means from their side. It is write-only input to generation and a check target for register, nothing else (dialogue-of-hades.md). A value that names a player feeling, sets a felt target, or reads as a score ("lands as grief, 0.8") is flagged on sight.

**The steering guard.** World Truths and Soul Arc Spines steer content and shape direction, structurally. A truth phrased as a player outcome, a spine treated as a required ending, or a delta phrased as an intended emotion is flagged. The spine biases which content exists; the bond still collapses only from what the player actually did.

#### 8.5.4 The Register

The register is Frieren-flat, and it is a production rule, not a mood board.

- One clause per line where possible. The 40-word dialogue ceiling is a cap, not a quota; most lines run far under it. Weight lands on a short trailing clause, one beat after the line looked finished (e01.md).
- Deflect, do not name. Grief, love, and awe arrive as logistics, a joke, or mild curiosity. A line may confirm a fact plainly. It never confirms a feeling (e02.md).
- Weight is preloaded, not performed. A payoff that was planted properly needs almost nothing; one word can carry it, and amplification destroys it (e14.md). Payoff lines get the tightest word ceiling in the game.
- Register locks per scene. One tone from the enum per scene, switching only at scene boundaries. The heaviest line uses the same short cadence as the jokes around it; the gap carries the weight (e04.md; e11.md). A verbose line tagged quiet is a defect no matter what its words say (e14.md).
- Character shows as repeated behavior and third-party notice. No soul ever states its own trait (e04.md). Effect shows before definition: the player witnesses the behavior before anyone explains it, and the explainer has standing to explain (e07.md).
- Cut before adding. "I will try" beats the longer version; what a soul is exact about, and vague about, does the characterization for free (writing-nothing-unsaid-words.md).
- Words are scarce on purpose. The gaps the player's imagination fills are chosen, and the same scene carries different weight depending on the bond the player brings to it: one authored scene, several meanings across timelines (harvesting-interactive-fiction.md). Withhold significance, never orientation: the player always knows why they are here, just not yet what it weighs (harvesting-interactive-fiction.md).

And the reconciliation rule, the one that makes the constraint checkable at every payoff: when a moment has earned amplification, the swell is visual or sonic, frame, light, sound, scale, and the text and voice layer stays flat or silent at that exact beat (voice-style-guide.md). The review question is concrete: did the swell land in the picture, or did the words just get bigger?

#### 8.5.5 The Build-Time Authoring Loop (ink)

Ink is the production engine, not a prototype to throw away (section 16). The loop runs on tools the sources document:

- The Inky editor carries the ink compiler built in and plays the story the moment it is written; its web export gives a free playable HTML build. That is the whole hot-reload loop, and it is enough (ink-syntax-reference.md).
- inklecate, the compiler Inky wraps, compiles .ink to .ink.json, and the compiled file is the one engine contract: Inky's web export runs it on inkjs in the browser POC, and inkcpp or Inkpot runs the same file in Unreal, the split the integration findings settle: prototype on web now, ship inkcpp, or Inkpot on UE 5.7 and up (ink-syntax-reference.md; ink-unreal-integration.md). No second format; the JSON handoff is the seam (arrow-narrative-tool.md).
- The graph goes up greybox first: knots and guards carrying placeholder lines and full branch logic. Read counts of visited content compile each payoff_condition into a guard on the payoff knot, so the story queries its own play history instead of a hand-kept flag table (ink-narrative-scripting-language.md; ink-syntax-reference.md; narrative-games-yarn-spinner.md). Gathers reconnect loose choice ends automatically; tunnels hold repeated rituals authored once and reused across souls; tags carry each line's metadata, id, speaker, tone, echo, canon, to the engine untouched (ink-narrative-scripting-language.md; ink-syntax-reference.md).
- Compiling is itself a first verification pass: the compiler warns on loose ends and ran-out-of-content flow, which catches dead ends and unfinished branches before a playtest (ink-syntax-reference.md). A small tag lint adds one static check on top: every line carries its required metadata tags, id, speaker, tone, canon. Presence only; the lint reads no meaning. Register and essence-vs-role are semantic judgments and belong to the Verifier (8.5.3), not to any script. The lint flags, never rewrites, same rule as the agents.
- Weave choices present as diegetic interactions, examine, go, ask, never a labeled menu of feelings; the tools' default button list is the one part not borrowed (arrow-narrative-tool.md; narrative-games-yarn-spinner.md).

The loop itself, run between playtest runs and never mid-run: take in the player background and the soul backstory guidelines; the Architect scaffolds the next branching sequence as greybox knots; Roc plays a run in Inky and edits prose inline, the same output gate as always, sitting inside the editor; the AI proposes the next beat set on request. Beat budget is watched the whole time: a manually decremented VAR tracks how many potential beats remain in the run under test, because ink counts what has been visited, not what remains (ink-syntax-reference.md, globals). That counter is an authoring aid only; if it ever ships in the build it becomes a progress meter over emotional content.

Three pieces do not exist yet and have to be built, stated plainly so nobody goes looking for them. First, the AI-in-the-editor is a small custom harness wrapping the ink compiler and runtime; it is not a shipped ink feature. Second, the tag lint is a custom script over the compiled JSON's tags; ink ships nothing like it. Third, cross-life persistence is host code: ink's own save serialization is fragile across authoring edits, read counts are saved only when used in logic and choice addresses renumber when a knot changes, so the project rule is no cross-session state inside ink, and the host serializes bond and knowledge state at run end and re-injects it next life (ink-syntax-reference.md). If that save is lost or truncated, every echo condition silently never fires and the whole recognition layer dies without an error, so this seam gets a test before any content depends on it.

#### 8.5.6 The Human Review Pass

Roc approves every line at the output. The Verifier's flags arrive as evidence; they are never a substitute for his judgment on whether the scene is any good. Three calls belong only to this gate. Whether a payoff's preload was sufficient to earn its swell is an editorial call; no agent self-certifies it (voice-style-guide.md). Whether a reshuffled role lands as "of course, that is who they are," a surprise without a twist, is a judgment of the landing, not the wording; the Verifier polices role-phrased traits (8.5.3, check 1) and this gate judges the effect (surprising-without-a-twist.md). And the SDT checklist runs here: autonomy, competence, relatedness. Autonomy and competence tend to arrive on their own from the deduction and verb systems. Relatedness is the easy miss, so it gets its own line on the checklist: did a soul get a relational beat this batch? The prior art earns that line the hard way; the campaign's own gap audit found its most important character had the widest distance between narrative weight and actual presence (D&D arc doc: NARRATIVE_NOTES.md).

SDT is a human checklist. No agent scores relatedness, resonance, or closeness. No pipeline field proxies for one, including any relabeling of the bond number. The checklist is questions Roc reads, not numbers the orchestrator computes.

#### 8.5.7 The Constraint, Stated

We write stories; we do not script payoff. Two things in this pipeline are scripted. The mechanical recognition: the player asserts a soul's essence, the game confirms at a threshold, wrong guesses revise and teach. And the text itself: every line authored through the process above and approved by a person. One thing is invited: what the player feels when a plain detail from another life turns out to have been load-bearing. We author content that makes room for that reaction. No agent produces it, no field records it, no metric measures it, and no scene claims it happened. Procedural technique is primary; cohesion and a compelling story are secondary, and both are in service of the invitation.
