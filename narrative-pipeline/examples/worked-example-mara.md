# Worked Example: one soul, two lives

Mara run through the pipeline (`../pipeline.md`) across a reshuffle. This shows how a persona_card, an echo_template, the cross-run canon store, the register, and the someone-else-voices-the-payoff shape resolve on one soul. It is illustrative, not shipped content.

## The card, abridged

**Mara.** Want: keep things from being lost. Behavior cluster: mends small broken things unasked; keeps a drawer of objects nobody claimed. Contrast: the player passes through lives, Mara holds on. Axes: deflects to logistics; exact about objects and prices, vague about people; warmth shows as effort. Suit: keeping. voice_register: short clauses, price-talk, never names a feeling. Conviction: the child's whistle in the drawer is not for sale. Arc-spine note (in the arc doc, not the card): keeper to giver. Locked canon: her sister died the winter before the player first arrived.

For contrast, a second card: **Odo.** Want: to be witnessed. Behavior cluster: retells the same three stories, one detail changed each year; deflects to jokes; warmth shows as accommodation. The two are not swappable by changing a noun.

## Life one, Mara is the blacksmith

Scene N delta: WORLD, the festival lanterns are folded from letters the dead never received; PERSONAL, Mara keeps the drawer. Her line, matter_of_fact: "Nails are three a copper. Leave the gate hinge, I did it this morning." The seed_event, seventeen words, planted beside a price: "Mara oils the school gate hinge before opening her stall, charges nobody, and does not mention it." speaker_intent: care routed through maintenance.

Scene N+1 delta: WORLD, the well rope has frayed another strand since last year (D&D arc doc: NARRATIVE_NOTES.md, show the change); PERSONAL, one drawer object is a child's whistle she will not sell. Both facts are new; neither restates.

## Life two, after the reshuffle, Mara is the postman

The canon store holds: the drawer travels with her, the sister is still dead, the prices are still exact. Her late line, same register, same clause economy: "Letters run late festival week. Leave the gate, I did it this morning." The near-verbatim recurrence is the motif shape (frieren-dialogue-jp.md).

The echo pays off once the payoff_condition is met: the player has confirmed her essence in a prior life and has read the seed. The payoff line belongs to a third party, the schoolteacher, flat: "That gate has never squeaked. Nobody ever knew who." Weight is assigned from someone else's attachment, at minimum length (e01.md; e14.md). No narration adds anything. If the moment earns a swell, the camera holds on the gate and the sound bed lifts; the text stays silent (voice-style-guide.md).

A life-two line that gives Mara a living sister, or a vague price, is a Verifier flag against her store.
