# persona_card Schema

One card per soul, filled by the Narrative Architect (§11 Agent 1) from Roc's seed plus its backstory guideline. **The canonical card lives at [`../../cast/<soul>.md`](../../cast/)** — one file per soul, and that is the file every later revision edits. A card also appears inside the dated folder of the run that generated it; those copies are frozen run artifacts and diverge as the cast card is amended. Built in step 3 of `../pipeline.md`. The field-level implementation schema is the crew's own build task; this is the GDD-altitude shape. Two tag classes: essence (invariant across all lives) and role (re-dealt each life). **The unit of reincarnation is a *life*, never a "run"** — the game's time model has no runs, and run-language in a spec the crew reads is how it reaches shipped content.

## How card fields are written (the card-prose register, ratified 2026-08-02 — Roc)

The card is not player-facing, so `../register.md` does not govern it — that file is scoped to the lines souls speak. This section is the card's own contract.

**The exemplars are `primal_seed` and any field marked Roc-authored verbatim.** Read those two before filling anything. They are what a card field sounds like.

The stakes: the orchestrator re-pins a compressed card summary and the `voice_register` block into **every** Content Agent call (`../pipeline.md` step 8), so the card's prose is the ambient style for every line generated against it.

The rules:

1. **Write complete sentences, not label-plus-gloss.** `Anticipation — supplies the thing before it is asked for` is the shape to avoid. The dash asserts a label and an example without ever stating the relation between them: it reads authoritative and specifies less than it appears to.
2. **State the mechanism, do not name it and gesture at it.** If a soul does not mention the thing he just did, the field says *why* he does not mention it. The reason is the load-bearing part; the label is not.
3. **Plain verbs. No nominalizations.** "He makes himself necessary," never "manufactures need for himself."
4. **Say the causal links out loud** — *so*, *because*, *which means*. Compressing a chain into a colon costs the reader the reasoning.
5. **No colon-into-triple-list, and no aphorism.** "His signature is tempo, never length" is a slogan, and slogans travel between cards. That is the same template-surfacing failure the `essence_descriptor` row below already flags, appearing in a field with no guard on it.
6. **Concrete nouns a person can picture.** Give the reader the test rather than the label: what he cannot answer if you ask him, what is already there when he reaches for it.
7. **Where a field genuinely needs a handle** (the `trait_axes` do), state the handle as its own short sentence and then explain it in full ones. Do not hang the explanation off a dash.
8. **Write failure modes, not verdicts.** "The common way this line goes wrong is the writer explaining the offer — cut everything after the comma" teaches the correction. "Appended reasons are a defect" only names a fence. The card owns craft; the Verifier owns verdicts — a prohibition names a region and pushes the model to the nearest adjacent template (`../pipeline.md` step 8 measured it: banning six constructions by name produced fourteen).
9. **Prefer an instance to a description, and an inventory to a ban.** Where a rule would forbid a construction, list the distinct moves that satisfy it instead and spend each once. One in-voice sample line teaches more than a paragraph describing the voice.

**Check both directions when filling a card.** A field must sound like this soul, and its *sentence shape* must not repeat the shape used for the same field on another card. Two souls sharing a field template is the schema surfacing, not two souls converging.

**The two fields re-pinned into every generation call are `essence_descriptor` and `voice_register`.** Their prose quality is therefore a production input, not documentation polish — which is what the card-prose register above is protecting.

## Pinned-context hygiene (added 2026-08-08 — Roc)

Only `essence_descriptor` and `voice_register` reach the generator. Everything a writer needs to *know* can live anywhere on the card; only what a writer should *sound like* may be pinned. Three rules, all mechanically checked by `../../tools/card-lint.mjs`:

1. **No verdict vocabulary in a pinned field.** The words *defect*, *barred*, *flag*, *flagged*, *violation*, and *check* may not appear in `essence_descriptor` or `voice_register`. A generator conditioned on a checker's vocabulary writes to avoid flags instead of writing to sound like a person. Move the sentence to `voice_enforcement` unchanged.
2. **Budgets.** `essence_descriptor` runs to 75 words, `voice_register` to 400. These ride into every call, so length here is paid again on every line the soul ever speaks. **A would-never/would-say table inside `voice_register` is not counted** (ruled 2026-08-08 — Roc): exemplar pairs are the most generative thing on the card and nothing should discourage adding them. The gate strips table rows before counting. A card needing more than 400 almost certainly has a mechanic stated twice rather than a soul with more depth. Em-dash discipline stays craft guidance under rule 1, not a check.

Run the gate after any card edit and before any generation pass:

```
node tools/card-lint.mjs
```

## Sample-line and invention safety (added 2026-08-25 — Roc, from local-model testing)

Two failure modes showed up across every local model tested against a retuned card (`pipeline-runs/2026-08-17-register-loosening/2026-08-24-local-model-findings.md`, `assignments/assignment-8-icm/_kobold-tests/round2-findings.md`), independent of quant or architecture — a card problem, not a model-selection problem:

1. **Verbatim sample-copying.** Every model tested reproduced at least one of `voice_register`'s "sounds like" lines back near-verbatim on a welcome/job-handoff-shaped scene. The fix that worked: pair the sample-line block with an explicit instruction that these lines are shape references, never to be output verbatim or near-verbatim — and apply it to **every** sample line on the card, not just the most-copied one (a partial fix left one model still leaking from an unedited line).
2. **Invented backstory for a named-but-unexplained figure.** A card that names someone without explaining them (Mara's "That was Ovin's, before") pulls models toward inventing a relationship or history for that name — three different models invented three different, mutually incompatible backstories for the same unexplained name. The fix: an explicit instruction that the named figure exists only as already written, with no new scenes, relationships, or explanation licensed.

Both belong in `voice_register` next to whatever they guard (the sample-line block, or a trait axis that names someone without explaining them) — not in `voice_enforcement`, since a generator needs to see the instruction, not just be checked against it after the fact.

## Role fields (re-dealt each life)

| Field | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `role_tag` | This life's job and everything downstream of it. Re-dealt at each reshuffle. |
| `age_band` | This life's age. **Role-side: re-dealt each life exactly like the job.** A soul is a pattern, not a station — and not an age either. Nothing on the essence side may depend on it; a "youthful" or "world-weary" trait is an essence-vs-role defect. Load-bearing for inter-relationships: who defers to whom, who is addressed as "boy" or "sir", who mentors, and which pairings the reshuffle makes newly strange when a soul who was elder last life is dealt a child this one. Without it the Content Agent invents age terms unlicensed and no check catches them. |

## Exceptions

| Field | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `authored_exceptions` | Marks a handwritten soul that breaks the schema on purpose (5 to 10 percent of the roster), so the Verifier reads the break as sanctioned. Records the in-character reason. |

## Not a card field

- **The thread registry.** A soul's *threads* — the open questions this life keeps feeding — live in a per-life sibling file, **`../../cast/[soul]-[role]-threads.md`** (ruled 2026-08-06, GP-92; [`../../gdd/07-cast.md`](../../gdd/07-cast.md)). Not on the card: threads are dealt with the role and do not survive the reshuffle, so a registry inside a reshuffle-invariant document reads as canon and quietly outlives the life it belongs to. **The Architect creates this file whenever a soul is dealt a role**; a carded soul with a role and no sibling registry is an incomplete deal. Shape: [`thread-registry-schema.md`](thread-registry-schema.md), checked by `../../tools/registry-lint.mjs`. Texture souls that own no threads record that on the card instead, as Bex's does — and **a texture soul's role-goal takes no thread row either** (ruled 2026-08-09): the rite, the letters and the harvest are `delta_situation`, referenced freely, never tracked.
- **The Soul Arc Spine.** The soul's "from X to Y" lives in the arc doc as a human note (`../steering-layer.md`), not on the card. It guides hand-seeding; it is not a machined parameter and has no scripting guard.
- **The bond score.** Accretes host-side, never on the card, never surfaced (`../pipeline.md`, step 9).

## Why these rules

<details>
<summary>Origin and history</summary>

- **The card-prose register (ratified 2026-08-02 — Roc)** — the first two cards drifted into a clinical write-up voice while the fields Roc wrote himself stayed plain, and nothing was checking the difference. A card in clipped em-dash analyst voice is a style exemplar for clipped em-dash output, which step 11 then has to strip back out.
- **Rule 8 / instances-over-bans** — `../pipeline.md` step 8: a shared voice reference produced six shared sentence constructions across four files; banning those six by name produced fourteen, the top one firing nine times. A prohibition supplies no gradient toward anything.
- **`essence_descriptor` redefinition (2026-08-03)** — until then it was the only essence field with no named consumer, which is exactly why it read as padding. The required/optional table was added the same day: nothing recorded which fields were load-bearing downstream, which is how a field with no consumer survived two cards.
- **`suit_tag` retirement (2026-08-08)** — where suit and belonging-stance disagreed the suit was wrong: it filed Nell with Toby under `giving` while the GDD frames Nell as the Giver's counter-voice, and filed Linnet with Mara under `keeping` while their stances differ in scope.
- **The 75/400 budgets (2026-08-08)** — the Architect's benchmarked ~60 cap (2026-07-25) did not survive contact: Toby's approved descriptor is 63, Bex's 62, so 75 is the benchmark plus the smallest honest allowance. `voice_register` was set at 300 from Toby and Bex, raised to 400 once Mara — seven distinct behaviours — came in at 377 after two genuine trims.
- **The em-dash ceiling, tried and dropped (2026-08-08)** — no one could say why the number should be six rather than four or ten, and an unjustified constant in a gate is worse than a stated preference.
- **The enforcement split (2026-08-08)** — enforcement prose inside `voice_register` was conditioning every Content call on *barred, defect, wrong, check*. Nothing was deleted in the split; the text moved. Bex's enforcement section is the form rule 8 was derived from and the standard other cards move toward.

</details>
