# Arc Doc Template

Copy this per arc (one arc = one span of reshuffle-lives, a season). Fill it before any scene generates. The concept behind each field is in `../steering-layer.md`. Everything here steers content or documents direction; nothing here names or targets a player feeling.

---

## Arc: [name] (lives [n] to [m])

### World Truths
Three to five hidden facts the arc steers toward. No scene states one; every scene serves one. Phrase each as a fact about the world, never as a player outcome.

### The Arc Question
One question the arc quietly answers through play. Never resolved in dialogue, never tested for comprehension.

### Soul Arc Spines
A human note, one line per soul, from X to Y across this arc. Guides which facets to surface early versus late. Not a card field, not a target; the bond still ends where the player takes it.
