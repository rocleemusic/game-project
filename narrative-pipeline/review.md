# The Human Review Pass

Roc approves every line at the output. The Verifier's flags arrive as evidence; they are never a substitute for his judgment on whether the scene is any good. Four calls belong only to this gate, because each is a judgment of the landing, not the wording, and no agent may measure it.

- **Did the preload earn the swell?** Whether a payoff's setup was sufficient to justify its amplification is an editorial call; no agent self-certifies it (voice-style-guide.md).
- **Did the reshuffle land?** Whether a reshuffled role reads as "of course, that is who they are," a surprise without a twist, is a judgment of the effect. The Verifier polices role-phrased traits (`guardrails.md`, check 1); this gate judges whether the swap works (surprising-without-a-twist.md).
- **Did the situation actually move, or only get re-worded?** (Ruled 2026-08-03 — Roc.) Situation is uncapped and stateful, so a thread can advance as often as the story wants: dough flat → starter begged off a neighbour → still twelve short is three situations, not one restated. But "still short of flour" re-declared as "flour is still short" is a scene adding nothing. Any threshold for how much change counts as a new state would be arbitrary and the Verifier cannot measure it, so this one is read here (`guardrails.md`, check 3). Note what is *not* being asked: referencing an established fact is free and uncapped — that is how threads stay connected, and it is never the defect. The question is only whether a **delta slot** earned its declaration.
- **The SDT checklist.** Autonomy, competence, relatedness. Autonomy and competence tend to arrive on their own from the deduction and verb systems. Relatedness is the easy miss, so it gets its own line: did a soul get a relational beat this batch? The prior art earns that line the hard way, the campaign's own gap audit found its most important character had the widest distance between narrative weight and actual presence (D&D arc doc: NARRATIVE_NOTES.md).

## SDT is a human checklist

No agent scores relatedness, resonance, or closeness. No pipeline field proxies for one, including any relabeling of the bond number. The checklist is questions Roc reads, not numbers the orchestrator computes.

Also at this gate: the Soul Arc Spine (`steering-layer.md`) is a human note, so if a spine is quietly being treated as a required ending rather than a seeding hint, this is where that is caught.
