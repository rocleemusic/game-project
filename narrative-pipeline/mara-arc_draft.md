# Mara — the Keeper: arc draft

**Draft for Roc's review, 2026-08-07.** The spine is already fixed in [`arc-festival-slice.md`](arc-festival-slice.md) line 43 — *clutch → transform; the bond re-forms, loss isn't final.* This drafts the machinery under it. Not carded: none of this belongs on `cast/mara.md`, which holds essence and voice only.

Everything here is Architect material and Roc's to rule. Nothing below names or targets a player feeling.

---

## The pressure source: time itself

**Toby's pressure is other people's needs. Ilsa's is arrivals against a count. Both need another character in the room to supply them.** Mara's doesn't, and that is the point of choosing it.

Keeping never finishes. The anchor-spot needs tending again every time it is tended. The drawer of unclaimed objects never empties, because unclaimed things keep arriving. And every year the festival runs, it runs one year further from the thing she is holding on to.

**Why this matters structurally:** a soul whose pressure needs a second party can only be moved when the scheduler puts that party on screen. Mara can be moved by the calendar alone. Across a reshuffle season where the cast is re-dealt every life, that makes her the one deep soul whose arc does not depend on who else got dealt in.

**The cost, stated plainly:** it also means she can be moved *without the player*, and an arc that advances while the player is absent is an arc the player did not have. The pressure supplies the pull; the player supplies whether it goes anywhere. If that split does not hold in practice, this is the part to cut.

## What transforms, and what does not

**Tending stays right.** The arc does not teach her that keeping was a mistake — she is not wrong to have kept, and a version that corrects her is the wrong story. What changes is *what the tending is for*.

- **Clutch:** the anchor holds the loss in place. Tending it is how the thing stays not-final.
- **Transform:** the anchor holds a connection that is still live. Tending it is how that connection gets fed.

Same behaviour on both sides. The drawer still gets kept, the corner is still set for two, the small broken things still get mended unasked. **Nothing observable changes** — which is what makes it hers rather than a conversion, and what keeps it inside her voice, where the past tense simply stops arriving uninvited.

**Never release.** She does not let go, she does not say goodbye, and no beat asks her to. This protects the parked letting-go ending; the archived guardrail stands.

## The two-state anchor — flagged, not designed

The archived Keeper notes carry a mechanic: the linked person is dealt **present or past** each life, and the anchor flips meaning accordingly — a shared connection when they are present, a reminder when they are not.

**This is the biggest open question in her arc and I have not drafted it, because it decides whether her arc is authored once or re-derived per life.** Two readings:

1. **Cross-life invariant.** The flip is a fact about the world, the same every season. Then it lands as a canon flag on her card and the Architect authors against it.
2. **Per-life variable.** The flip is re-rolled with the deal. Then the arc must be written to play in both states, and the authored beats roughly double.

Roc ruled 2026-08-07 that the lost person is **her sister**, which settles who — not whether the sister can be dealt present. Those are separate rulings and the second is still open.

## What this arc is NOT

- **Not a grief arc that resolves.** The loss does not get processed, discussed, or worked through. `voice_register` bars long runs about the loss absolutely; an arc that needs one is the wrong arc.
- **Not a rescue.** The player does not fix her, and no beat is built where they succeed at that. Her recognition hook — finding the beauty in what is passing — is already functioning, not a symptom.
- **Not Toby's shape.** Toby's belief flips when the player claims him unearned. Mara's does not flip and is not triggered by an act of the player's. It re-aims, slowly, and the player is a witness to that rather than its cause.
- **Not Ilsa's shape either.** Ilsa's arc qualifies a belief she keeps. Mara's keeps the behaviour and changes what it serves — the inverse.
