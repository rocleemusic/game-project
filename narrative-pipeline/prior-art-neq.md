# NeverEndingQuest — prior art read against this pipeline

NeverEndingQuest (NEQ) is the closest running public system to what we are building: one orchestrator LLM plus a dozen single-purpose specialist calls, all flowing through code-validated JSON schemas, engineered to run a campaign forever without the context window exploding. The full code-read is in [`../knowledge-base/ai-workflow/neverendingquest-ai-dm-architecture.md`](../knowledge-base/ai-workflow/neverendingquest-ai-dm-architecture.md). This note reads its mechanisms against our pipeline and decides, one at a time, what we take.

**The frame, applied to every pattern below.** NEQ is an AI DM improvising live in real time. We are an AI-assisted authoring pipeline: generate ahead, hand-check, human-gate, then play. Same building blocks, opposite posture. A mechanism that is load-bearing for live improvisation is not automatically useful for authoring, so every pattern is filtered before it is taken.

## The three buckets

- **ADOPT** — posture-independent. Works the same whether we author ahead or improvise live. Take it.
- **ADAPT** — good pattern, wrong shape. Reshape it for authoring plus the human gate before taking it.
- **MIRROR** — the road §8.5 deliberately refuses. Keep only as a cited contrast that sharpens why we refuse it. Do not build it.

## Adopt — what folds in

- **The NPC codex, as a specced artifact (pattern 2).** We already say the Architect cannot invent a soul and that `canon_flags` lock what must not drift. NEQ shows the missing piece: a single canonical registry, built once per arc from the roster of seeds, that the Verifier reads every batch as the source of truth for "does this soul exist and are its locked facts intact." Proposed home: a new invariant line in `guardrails.md` naming the codex as the registry check 4 runs against, and a one-line note in `pipeline.md` step 2 that Intake emits the codex when the roster is set.
- **The authored-vs-generated template (pattern 5).** NEQ's `module_builder` is a concrete list of which steps are LLM-authored and which are code-built. Our step 6 asserts "graph before prose" but does not enumerate the code-built half. Proposed home: a short enumerated split in `pipeline.md` step 6 (LLM authors the scene spec and branch intent; code builds node IDs, gating conditions, the zero-information fallback, and the backup), citing NEQ as the running template.

## Adapt — reshaped for our posture

- **The action contract (pattern 1).** NEQ's boundary is exactly ours ("cross-run state lives in the engine save, not the narrative layer"), but NEQ draws it as typed `actions[]` a handler dispatches. We can borrow the shape for step 8/9: the Content Agent emits line text only; any state change (bond accretion, canon write, reshuffle role) is a typed host-side action, never a value the narrative layer sets. Reshape note: unlike NEQ we apply these at build/author time behind the human gate, not live, so there is no runtime validator between narration and dispatch. Proposed home: tighten `build-loop.md` on where state actions live, cross-referencing step 9.
- **Compression threshold (pattern 4).** Replace step 8's "re-summarize accumulated scene state every ten slots" framing with a named threshold-and-compress rule modeled on chronicle compression: at N accumulated slots, compress the oldest M into one running summary the orchestrator re-pins. Keeps our number, borrows NEQ's explicitness. Proposed home: a one-line sharpening in `pipeline.md` step 8.
- **Retry policy (pattern 6).** NEQ's ≤5-retry loop is a working answer to our open escalation question, so we specify the policy rather than parking it. We do not want silent auto-retry (it hides drift from the gate), so the adapted rule is a *capped, explicit* revision count on prose flags: up to N revisions inside the same canon, and the flag rides to the human gate if still unresolved after N, with model fallback allowed on repeated failure. Proposed home: `pipeline.md` step 13, replacing the bare "one revision, then gate."
- **Relational index (pattern 7, index half).** NEQ's `module_context.json` is a per-module relational index cross-referencing areas ↔ NPCs ↔ plot, kept separate from the quest DAG. Adapted to our posture it is a canon-coherence index that binds each soul's locked facts to the places and threads they touch, which is the data the NPC codex (row 2) reads against. Proposed home: fold into the codex spec rather than as its own artifact, cross-referenced from `guardrails.md` check 4.
- **Bond scoring, not stored felt-state (pattern 8, adapt half).** NEQ scores relationship affect as stored per-NPC vectors; we reject that (see Mirror). What we take is the *scoring* idea, made §8.5-safe. Two moves:
  - **Richer orthogonal card traits.** Widen step 3's fixed, orthogonal trait axes (today: deflection / precision / warmth) and lean harder on suit-tagging, per `modular-characters-system-driven.md`. Pure characterization, essence-side, no bond coupling. Proposed home: `pipeline.md` step 3.
  - **A weighted bond delta.** An incoming player action produces **one** delta to the single hidden bond count (step 9). Its weight is a function of two inputs: a small hand-set of **named action-categories** the act falls under, and the soul's **fixed card-trait coefficients** (a guarded soul's bond moves slower). The category set, locked with Roc: **Trust** (kept a promise or secret, reliability), **Intimacy** (shared time, disclosure, presence), **Recognition / being-seen** (the player notices and remembers a soul across a reshuffle, the game's thematic core), **Respect** (competence or craft shown, a soul's values honored). Fear and power are dropped as off-pillar. Each category must be reachable through a verb family (Collect / Make / Use / Converse, with Show and Ask), because bond counts what the player *did*, not what the soul feels. Proposed home: `pipeline.md` step 9 plus the bright line in `guardrails.md`.
  - **The bright line (guardrail):** the bond is fed by a scoring *function*; it is never *split into stored sub-scores*. Trust / Intimacy / Recognition / Respect name **action-categories that weight a single delta**, never a **persisted per-soul dimension**. The moment a second hidden number is stored per soul, it is NEQ's 5-D model and the Superposition guard (`guardrails.md` check 2) flags it. Card traits act only as fixed coefficients; they never accrue.

## Mirror — the §8.5 refusals, cited

Two NEQ systems are the concrete form of the two roads §8.5 refuses. They are most useful cited at the exact place we state the refusal, so the discipline reads as a choice against a real alternative rather than an assertion.

- **Scripted plot DAG, sequence half (pattern 7)** → cite at `pipeline.md` step 6 and/or `guardrails.md`, next to "encounters, not quests." (The index half of pattern 7 adapts; see Adapt above.)
- **Quantified emotion, stored-felt-state half (pattern 8)** → cite at the Superposition guard (`guardrails.md` check 2) and step 9, next to "bond is a hidden count, never a number." Tie the citation to the `primal-world-beliefs-npc-lens.md` watch-out: NEQ's 5-D relationship vectors are the concrete form of the "flatten NPCs into numbers" failure that note warns against. The two systems are orthogonal to each other, primals model a soul's *worldview* (essence-side, our persona_card), NEQ's vectors model *feeling toward the player* (bond-side); NEQ is the picture of measuring the second, which we refuse. (The scoring half of pattern 8 adapts; see Adapt above. What mirrors is storing the affect as persisted per-soul dimensions, not the act of weighting a single delta.)

Neither is built. Both are read for contrast only.

## Rejected

- **The validator-layer (pattern 3).** We do not add an LLM validation call between generation and the human gate. NEQ runs stacked LLM validators but ships anyway (the open Thornwood issue); our deterministic Verifier plus the human gate is the hard block, and an LLM validator would add cost while hiding drift the gate is meant to catch. Decision made here, not left open. If it ever reopens it is a §20 design decision, but the retry policy (pattern 6) is where the equivalent effort goes.
