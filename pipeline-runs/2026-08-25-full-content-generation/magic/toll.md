# toll

**Category:** spell

**OLD description:** none — new field

**NEW description (generated):**

The priest strikes the metal or stone with a wooden mallet to sound a single, true tone. It is a deep, carrying sound that vibrates in the air. When cast at high mana, the note rings clear and carries across the village, but at baseline it stays close, heard only by those nearby. It is used to open the Lantern Arch at the Festival Grounds.
