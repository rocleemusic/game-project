# waft

**Category:** spell

**OLD description:** none — new field

**NEW description (generated):**

Waft sends smoke, scent, or dust rising in a straight column. To cast it, you hold a handful of the substance and direct your attention upward. A well-cast spell keeps the column unbroken and tall, but at baseline the edges fray and the height barely reaches a body's height. It is best for seeing movement in the air or letting a scent travel.
