# echo

**Category:** spell

**OLD description:** none — new field

**NEW description (generated):**

The sound comes back from the stone or the wood, a bit thinner than when it was spoken. It does not need a voice or a mouth, only a hard surface to hold the echo. If there is enough mana, the sound is clear and full, but with less it is muffled and short, like hearing a person through a wall. It is not a new sound, just the old one, played back to where it is.
