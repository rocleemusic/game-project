# ignite

**Category:** spell

**OLD description:** none — new field

**NEW description (generated):**

The flame is not a fireball. It starts as a small, hot spark that catches on dry brush, paper, or cloth, turning it into a steady burn. At high mana, the heat is cleaner and more controlled, making a better blaze. At low mana, it is just a little sputter of light. It is good for starting fires, but not for fighting.
