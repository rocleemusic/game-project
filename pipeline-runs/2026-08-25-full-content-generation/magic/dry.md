# dry

**Category:** spell

**OLD description:** none — new field

**NEW description (generated):**

The spell pulls moisture from the center of an object without affecting the outside. It looks like the water just settles out and pools on the ground, leaving the thing it was inside dry. If you use enough mana, the thing comes out flat and as it was before. If you use less, it dries a bit stiff and curled at the edges. It is a good one to have on hand when traveling in the rain.
