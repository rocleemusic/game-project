# leap

**Category:** spell

**OLD description:** none — new field

**NEW description (generated):**

To cast leap, you reach for a flame and send it toward a prepared wick. The flame travels through the air as a single streak, jumping from wick to wick as it goes. When your mana is high, the flame runs far and lights many wicks in one go, but at baseline it only crosses to the nearest prepared wick. Each leap consumes the flame you sent.
