# sift

**Category:** spell

**OLD description:** none — new field

**NEW description (generated):**

A thin, rhythmic pulsing in the hands that shakes the air, separating the fine from the coarse in a mixture. It makes a sound like sand sliding through a sieve. For most grains, one pass leaves a few stubborn bits behind, but for a second-pass-perfect split, the hands must move with more force. It is good for flour, or for finding metal in soil.
