# rest

**Category:** spell

**OLD description:** none — new field

**NEW description (generated):**

I sit with the baker and watch the loaves. It feels like holding my breath. A small, still weight settles on the counter and keeps the bread from changing. If the mana is high, the bread stays soft and warm as if just pulled from the oven, and the moment stays held. When the mana is low, the hold is not as firm and the bread slowly hardens or cools. The spell only pauses things; it does not make them new.
